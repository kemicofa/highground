# Highground — PvP Command Board

Highground is a PvP addon for World of Warcraft Retail, WoW Forever and Cataclysm Classic. It shows who to hit, what the objectives are doing and who is around you, so you spend less time reading the UI and more time winning.

## Features

### ⚔️ Battlegrounds

- **Enemy list:** every enemy with its kill priority and spec, which you can tag when the game hides it. Left-click to target, right-click to focus.
- **Target and healer markers:** a sight over your recommended target and crosses over enemy and friendly healers.
- **Objective timers:** graveyard waves, base capture countdowns and a win forecast; shift-click one to share it in chat.
- **Flag carriers and map intel:** carrier health and debuffs, plus where enemies were last seen as circles on the map, shared with teammates.
- **Quick callouts and history:** one-click calls to battleground chat, and saved results for every match.

### 🎯 Arena

- **Enemy team:** clickable opponents with spec icons, casts and crowd control.
- **PvP trinkets:** see when each opponent's trinket is back.
- **Opt in:** Arena starts off; switch it on in the Highground window's Addons page.

### 🌍 Open world

- **Nearby enemies:** hostile players seen by you, your group, your guild or other Highground users, with a live count and stealth alerts.
- **Enemy heatmap:** the world map shades where enemies were seen and who won the fights there in the last hour.
- **Tracking and marks:** track an enemy to hear where they turn up, and mark gankers or calls for help on the map.
- **Kill order in world fights:** in a party or raid, kill order, health bars and healer markers cover the enemies around you.
- **Enemy journal:** who killed you and who you killed, with notes, muting and tracked and bounty filters.

### 💰 Bounties (alpha)

- **Wanted list:** put a bounty on any player, with or without a gold reward.
- **Wanted alerts:** a poster and sound when your target shows up.
- **Boards:** keep bounties private, or share them with your guild, a community or everyone.
- **Suggestions:** spot players who keep killing you or your guild.

### 🛡️ Reputation (alpha)

- **Player reports:** your guild or community flags problem players. See flags in tooltips and optionally filter their chat. Officers and moderators review reports.

### 🧙 Macros

- **Ready-made macros:** PvP and PvE macros for your class: interrupts, crowd control, mouseover heals and more.
- **Builder:** write your own; each line is explained in plain English and mistakes are flagged as you type.
- **Spell ranks:** on WoW Forever, buttons and macros move to your highest rank.

### 🪟 Highground window

- **One window:** settings, previews and tools behind `/hg` or the minimap button.
- **Modules:** switch Battlegrounds, Arena, Nearby, Bounties, Reputation and Macros on or off.
- **Make it yours:** six themes, alert presets and sounds, and shareable profiles.
- **Languages:** English and nine other languages.

### 🔥 Extra on Cataclysm Classic

- **Enemy cooldowns:** trinkets, defensives and big offensives on enemy rows and in target priority.
- **Burst markers:** an icon beside an enemy using a big cooldown, marked PURGE or CC.
- **More detail:** health percentage, mana bars and pressure shields on teammates.

## Getting started

1. Install every folder in the Highground ZIP and restart WoW.
2. Type `/hg` or click the minimap button.
3. Run `/hg setup` to pick a playstyle and place your panels.
4. Try `/hg test` to preview the panels outside a match.

Formerly Warboard: delete the old `Warboard` addon folder.
