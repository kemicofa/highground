## The Highground window

`/hg`, the minimap button, the **Open or close Highground** key binding and **Open Highground** in **Options → AddOns → Highground** all open the same window. It works in combat, moves by its header and closes with Escape. Each time you open it, it starts on **Home**, or the page a command or menu asks for, with fresh **Back** and **Forward** history. Guides, panel placement, dialogs and tool windows opened from it bring you back to the same page when you close them, press Escape or choose **Done**. **Reload to apply** reopens it on the same page. **Open** buttons launch each addon's tools in their own windows: macros, Reputation, the bounty board, the enemy journal and match history. Blizzard's settings panel shows the same settings, appearance and preview pages. The minimap button's right-click menu offers lock/unlock, Highground, Addons, macros, the bounty board, Reputation and help. Drag the button around the minimap, or turn it off with **Minimap button** in **Shared preferences → General**. Retail and WoW Forever also list Highground in the AddOns button beside the minimap.

The sidebar has:
- **Home**: which panels are enabled, **Back to game**, **Preview and move panels**, and shortcuts to addons and alerts.
- **Addons** (`/hg guide`, also `/hg checklist`): each addon's **Enable addon** switch and guide. The switch is the same per-character setting as WoW's AddOn List; **Reload to apply** starts or stops the addons you changed, and switching back before reloading cancels the change. Settings and records are kept. Nearby and Arena also have a separate **Show panel** switch. **Visit** opens the addon's overview. If old standalone downloads are installed, a notice lists exactly which folders to remove; keep both required data addons enabled.
- **Battlegrounds**, **Arena**, **Nearby**, **Bounties**, **Reputation** and **Macros**: each addon's own section, marked with its crest, with **Overview and enable**, **Settings**, **Previews and guide**, **Commands**, and **Appearance** for Battlegrounds, Arena, Nearby and Bounties. Each section owns its feature switches, alert categories and panel controls.
- **Shared preferences**: **General**, **Shared appearance** and **Alerts**: minimap button, master scale and sound, language, theme, accessibility, shared HUD styling and the overall alert level. **Language** (`/hg config general`) defaults to English. Choose another WoW language or **Use game language**; the choice applies to every character and is not part of profiles. Text without a translation stays in English, and slash commands and game spell names are unchanged.
- **Saved setups**: **Playstyle presets** and **Profiles** (settings per character and addon). Addon enable switches are not part of profiles.
- **Help**: **Addon users** and **Help and commands**.

### Guides

Each guide uses the real controls with short explanations, highlights and a moving pointer (off with Reduced motion). **Guides**, **Replay**, **Back** and **Next** share one row, and each guide saves its place. **Next** always works: when a step asks you to click something, Next clicks it for you, and moves on if the sample refuses the click. Each card shows **In progress** or **Completed**, and finishing a guide says so. If a guide can't start (in combat, in a match, or with all its panels off), the reason appears on its card. Every step of a feature's guide shows that feature's switch, which saves its real preference immediately and also turns on any parent feature it needs. Steps about an alert add **Play this sound in game**, **Play sound** and a sound choice; turning the sound on also turns on its alert, and the guide says when your alert level still mutes it. Sample records stay separate from yours; real settings and macro saves are labelled.

- `/hg guide battlegrounds` offers **Enemy rows & targeting** (`/hg guide enemies` or `/hg guide targeting`) and **Objectives & flag carriers** (`/hg guide objectives`).
- `/hg guide bounties` offers **Post a bounty** (`bounty_post`: creation, claim review and payment records) and **Hunt a bounty** (`bounty_hunt`: discovery, tracking, kill reports and your own claims). Each follows one sample target; **Back** retraces your steps.
- Nearby's guide covers **History → select an enemy → Add bounty**. `/hg guide reputation` follows one sample report from submission through moderation, then pausing participation and your preferences.
- **More guides** in Addons covers shared topics such as the enemy map, Quick callouts, appearance, alerts and saved setups. **How addons work** (`/hg guide addons`) explains installation, the switches and reloads.

### Addon users

**Help → Addon users** (`/hg users`) counts Highground users you can see: your character, the latest complete member list of the discovery channel, and announcements received in the last 15 minutes. Each character counts once. Opening the page or pressing **Refresh members** requests a member list; requests are throttled and wait while WoW's channel UI is open or messaging is locked. A channel member without an announcement is counted separately: they may not have a responding addon. If a new list is incomplete or unavailable, the previous complete list stays for up to six minutes, then the page falls back to announcements. Counts reset on reload and are a sample, not a server census.

Detection and realm discovery have separate switches. A newer release reported by a guild or group member, or by two characters in the realm channel, shows one notice per session, at most once a day; check it in your addon manager. **Copy diagnostics** (`/hg users why`) reports messaging restrictions, channel membership, roster status and retries without sending messages. `/hg guide users` shows the page with sample members. See [discovery details](../../../docs/networking.md#addon-user-discovery).

### Moving panels and previews

Use **Preview and move panels** on Home. Sample panels appear outside a match; drag their headers and click **Done** to return to the game. Positions save automatically. You can also unlock panels from Home, the minimap menu or any panel's cogwheel; move them outside combat, then lock them. Unlocking shows headers even in Frameless mode.

Every separate HUD panel has a cogwheel for lock/unlock, arrangement, quick actions and settings. A compact battleground column shares one cog on its first visible panel. Locked cogs stay faint and brighten when you hover the panel; they stay bright while unlocked or while their menu is open, including in Frameless mode. In combat, placement waits until combat ends.

`/hg bg test` (or **Battlegrounds → Previews and guide → Battleground panels**) toggles sample enemy rows, resurrection timers, the win forecast, timers and flag carriers with your settings; `/hg bg test stop` stops them. `/hg test` starts the samples of every enabled addon and keeps any already running; repeat it, or use `/hg test stop`, to stop them all. **Help and commands** has the same start and stop controls. Start previews outside combat, matches, guides and panel placement; any of those ends them. Bounties shows its wanted-poster sample; Macros and Reputation use their guides and real tools instead. See [preview commands](commands.md#previews).

## Features

Open **Settings** in the addon’s own sidebar section. All available switches and detailed controls are shown together; scroll to reach the rest. The addon’s **Overview and enable** switch controls whether the whole addon runs after reload. Disabled child switches explain which parent to enable. Every change saves immediately.

New installations include unrated battlegrounds, show the whole enemy team, enable the target crosshair and Nearby enemies, and turn on every alert's text and sound. Nearby starts with five rows. Burst markers, pressure shields, objective overview, sap calls and low-health resurrection reminders start off. The quest tracker stays visible. Upgrades enable unrated battlegrounds once for the account and saved setups, preserving other preferences. Later scope choices are kept. Shared-bounty receiving and publishing remain separate opt-ins.

**Battleground sizes:** every Battlegrounds page starts with four size tabs, **10v10** (rated battlegrounds and Blitz too, where the game has them), **15v15**, **Epic** (Ashran too) and **Open world** (open-world fights). Each size keeps its own copy of every Battlegrounds setting: switches, appearance, panel positions and scales, alerts, targeting and scoring. The page edits the selected size only, and the note under the tabs says which. Every other way to change a battleground setting edits that same size and names it: slash commands ("Compact layout on (15v15 battlegrounds)"), guide switches, playstyle presets and the panels' gear menu (**Editing: 15v15 battlegrounds**). During a match you edit and see that match's size (**This match**), during a world fight Open world (**This fight**); other sizes wait until it ends. `/hg sizes [10|15|40|world]` shows or picks the size. **Use Highground in…** on each size (`/hg sizes 10|15|40|world on|off`) says where Highground runs; where the game has rated battlegrounds, 10v10 adds **Include unrated 10v10 matches** (`/hg all`); WoW Forever has none, so its 10v10 switch covers every match. Open-world fights need Nearby. A battleground Highground doesn't know yet uses the game's player limit. **Preview** shows that size's sample panels (10, 15 or 40 enemies; for Open world, Nearby's list in fight mode) where they will appear, unlocked: drag them, change settings and watch them update; switching size switches the samples, and closing the window ends it (`/hg sizes preview`). **Copy to other sizes…** (`/hg sizes copy 10|15|40|world …`) overwrites the sizes you tick with this one's settings; nothing else ever changes several sizes. **Sounds** mutes every Highground sound. Unsupported controls are hidden for your client.

### Set up for your role

**Saved setups → Playstyle presets** (`/hg setup`) offers **Minimal**, **Damage**, **Healer**, **Flag carrier** and **Caller**. **Use [playstyle] setup** changes the battleground tools and battleground alerts of the size you edit (the window names it), then opens panel placement. Save a named profile first if you want a reusable copy of your current setup. Other choices, such as nearby enemies, arena panels and observation sharing, stay as you set them. **Move current panels** opens placement with your current settings.

Placement shows example data and draggable headers. Choose another panel set to arrange arena or nearby panels too. **Done** saves positions, locks panels and returns to the game. Closing placement, entering combat or joining a match stops previews and restores the earlier lock state. Named profiles are optional; ordinary settings always save automatically.

### Alert controls

Written alerts have a **Text** switch, and every alert has a **Sound** switch. **Target lock** and **Kill window** are sound-only cues, so they have no Text switch. Each available switch decides what the alert shows or plays. `/hg alerts` offers presets that set all of them at once: **Everything** (the new-install default), **Critical only**, **Objectives** and **Text only** (every sound off); `/hg alerts preset everything|critical|objectives|quiet` does the same. After a preset, changing one switch shows **Custom**; a preset keeps nothing of its own. **Sounds** in Shared preferences mutes every sound; while it is off, Sound switches are greyed out and say so.

Under **Addon alerts**, the **Battlegrounds**, **Nearby** and **Bounties** quick links open each addon's settings at its text and sound switches. Links for disabled addons become available after enabling the addon and reloading. **Enemy stealth** is directly available on the Alerts page because it covers several addons. Changes save automatically. Sounds arriving together use the highest-priority cue; **Sounds** in Shared preferences mutes them all. **Play sound** beside an alert auditions its cue even when Highground alerts are muted, without triggering an event or changing your preferences; WoW sound settings still apply. Enemy stealth offers entering and leaving cues. Each alert in an addon's settings has a **Sound** choice: its own Highground cue by default, any other Highground cue, or a game sound such as Raid warning. Choosing one plays it once; `/hg alerts sound ALERT SOUND` does the same. The **Target lock** sound choice picks the lock-on cue; its Sound switch mutes it (`/hg sniper sound 0`, while `/hg sniper sound N` picks cue N and turns the switch on). Target lock plays as soon as the marker moves to a new enemy, even right after another alert sound.

**Enemy stealth** uses altered versions of the game's stealth sound: lower-pitched when applied, higher-pitched when removed. This works for nearby enemies, active battlegrounds and arenas. Nearby also follows its own sound switch. Cata reads applications and removals from the combat log; Retail and Forever use readable enemy casts and reappearance; Nearby also detects readable hiding auras. Restart WoW after installing the new sound files.

Base capture, objective changes, resurrection and sap calls each have their own cue: a two-tone siren, a falling horn call, rising bells and soft plucks. Until WoW is restarted after installing them, these play the raid-warning sound.

## Main panel

Enemy rows keep their positions by default while priority ranks and recommendations update. The initial roster is alphabetical; new arrivals append without moving existing enemies. Death, resurrection, range and priority changes do not reorder the list. Enable **Battlegrounds → Target behavior → Reorder enemy rows by priority (out of combat)** to make rows follow priority instead. Secure click bindings always stay fixed during combat.

The **suggested-target card** above the list is off by default. Enable **Hero card**, the first switch under **Battlegrounds → Appearance → Enemy rows**, to show the recommended enemy's name, class, health and teammate pressure, and **on you** or **on healer NAME** when that enemy targets you or one of your team healers. Hiding the card keeps priority calculations, row highlights and recommendations active.

New installations show every enemy; change **Battlegrounds → Appearance → Enemies shown** or use the cogwheel to show five, ten or all enemies. Saved setups that kept the earlier five- or ten-row default were moved to all. A header such as **5/15** means five of fifteen are displayed; **· 3 ON YOU** follows while three enemies in view target you. With stable ordering, a higher-priority enemy can be outside that limit; choose **All** to see the whole roster. Hover a row for the full spec and how it was found, a favorable or unfavorable matchup, location, cast, and cooldowns used with time remaining. Numerical scores are available in **Target behavior → Refresh comparison**.

### Enemy row legend

Some elements depend on your client, current observations and Appearance switches. Compact layout hides the priority tag and teammate count.

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Class-coloured background and narrow strip | The enemy's class. Highground and Class Colors keep the whole row tinted independently of health; Class Colors uses stronger tinting. |
| Number at the left | **Priority rank**, with 1 highest; this is not a score or a fixed row number. Ranks update while row positions stay fixed. By default they use base priority; the live-score option includes current combat signals. A brief green/red flash means the rank improved/worsened. |
| Name | Enemy player, with the local realm omitted. Other realms remain visible except on Forever, which hides realm suffixes. A faded name means no current unit observation. A long name extends over empty slots on its right and stops before anything the row shows. |
| Icon | Specialization when known; otherwise class. It becomes grey when dead. |
| Skull, cross or other marker over the icon | The team's raid target marker. A skull or cross raises the enemy's priority. WoW Forever doesn't allow raid markers on enemy players, so its mark key pings instead. |
| Spec text, such as `FROST`, `DISC` or `ARMS` | Short specialization name, only when the icon can't show it: **Spec icon** is off (Battlegrounds → Appearance), or the game draws a spec addons can't read. `~` before the spec means estimated: on Cata, or from a druid's forms on WoW Forever. Green/red indicates a favorable/unfavorable modeled matchup for your spec; muted text is neutral. With the icon shown, the tooltip names the matchup instead. |
| Spec icons at the right edge (while hovering) | Shown when the game hasn't identified the spec. Click one to tag it; click the tagged icon again to remove the tag. Teammates with Highground receive tags while **Share observations** is on (Battlegrounds → Settings → Map and sharing), and tags are kept for later matches. WoW Forever blocks addon messages for the whole match, so it has no **Share observations** switch and tags stay yours. A spec the game identifies this match (scoreboard, a talent-tree cast, or a Cata estimate) replaces a tag; a tag replaces earlier matches' history. The tooltip says who tagged it. |
| `CAP 3.2` / `CAP ~3.2` | Capturing a base, with seconds remaining; `~` marks an estimated timer. Shown in the spec text's place. |
| `FLAG`, `FLAG x3`, `ORB 30%` | Carrier information replaces the spec: flag carrier, flag debuff stacks, or extra damage taken from carrying an orb. |
| Health bar, fill or thin line | Health when observable (**Health style** in Battlegrounds → Appearance: a bar under the name by default, the whole row, or a thin line). A muted bar is the last health seen before the enemy left view, kept for **Keep last seen health** (Target behavior, 15 s by default); a faded full bar means unknown health, not confirmed full health; a dead enemy's bar is empty. Teammates' targets count as in view while your client can see them. In the normal Bar layout, range and priority use fitted columns beside health; every row keeps the same track width. Rows grow when thicker bars or larger text need more room. |
| `TGT`, `FOC`, `T/F` | Your target, focus, or both. An ivory target edge and blue focus edge remain separate from class identity and the gold recommendation. Labels sit below the rank in expanded rows; compact rows keep the edges. |
| `72%` | Health percentage, at the end of the bar or beside the name in other styles. Muted for a last-seen reading. On Cata it turns red at or below the execute threshold; Retail and WoW Forever show the game's number without Highground reading it, so the red execute outline marks that threshold there. |
| Thin blue bottom bar | Mana for observed mana users (Cata); fades as the reading gets older. |
| Thin top bar | Cast/channel progress: amber if interruptible, grey if not. Also shows capture progress; an estimated capture bar is faded. |
| Plain range number, such as `30` | Nearest confirmed spell-range band in yards, not an exact distance. Bright within reach, muted beyond reach. `FAR` means visible without a confirmed range band; `--` means unknown. A green dot can display an in-range answer when the game hides its value. |
| `NOW` | The current reachable attack recommendation, also marked by an accent outline and rank tile. |
| `TOP` | Highest-ranked living enemy when someone else is the current attack recommendation. Older settings text called this `PRIO`. |
| `DEAD` | Enemy is dead; the row is dimmed and stays in place. |
| PvP trinket icon with a number | Cata trinket tracking: bright means ready or not observed used; grey plus a number means estimated seconds until ready. |
| `x3` beside the spec | Three teammates currently targeting this enemy. Highlighted from three upward; different from `FLAG x3` stacks. |
| Eye icon | This enemy is targeting you. On WoW Forever, Highground matches the name of the enemy's target, because the game hides whether it is you. The header adds **· 3 ON YOU** while enemies in view target you. |
| Thin pale outline and arrow outside the row | Your currently selected target. This can differ from the recommended target. |
| Red edge/glow | An attack opening after defensive protection ends. A separate red outline indicates health under the execute threshold; reduced motion makes animated cues steady. |
| Gold outline and coin | An active bounty on this enemy, when bounty markers are enabled. |
| Dimmed row | Dead enemies are strongly dimmed; defensive protection, immunity or preserved crowd control also dims content. An enemy lost while its combat click is frozen is faded further. |

The **status chip** shows one state at a time, in this precedence order:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **IMMUNE** | Known full immunity; never recommended |
| **DEF** | Defensive active |
| **CC** | Breakable crowd control when specifically identified, or a general crowd-control observation |
| **STUN/SIL** | Stunned or silenced (Cata) |
| **FLAG / ORB** | Flag or orb carrier |
| **SPENT** | Defensive protection recently ended; the attack opportunity is fading |
| **PURGE** | Offensive cooldown your class can remove (Cata) |
| **BURST** | Offensive cooldown active |

The specific breakable-CC observation takes precedence over `STUN/SIL`; a generic CC observation follows it. On WoW Forever, which refuses addons enemy buffs and debuffs, the generic **CC** comes from the game's loss-of-control count for an enemy in view: it says the enemy is stunned, feared, sapped or otherwise controlled right now, not which kind or for how long, so it neither protects breakable control nor scores as a stun. Retail and WoW Forever restrict enemy health and power readings: Highground can show the game's health bar, percentage and green-to-red colour but cannot use health for scoring, so mana, health scoring and combat-log trinket countdowns are Cata features. See [estimated specs](observations.md#estimated-specs-cata) for how Cata estimates specialization.

Left-click an enemy row to select it; right-click sets your focus, a second target kept separately from your current target. Shift-click calls that enemy in chat. The card's name is its click target and takes the same shift-click. Outside combat, the card and the target key follow the recommendation. In combat, click bindings cannot change: if the recommendation changes, the card shows **CLICK TARGET · FIXED IN COMBAT** with **RECOMMENDED: name** below it, so use that enemy's row or nameplate. With no target bound before combat, the card shows **Target click unavailable** until combat ends. An attack opening occurs when an enemy's observed defensive protection ends: the card shows **ATTACK · PROTECTION ENDED** and the alert can play a sound.

### Markers over the world

- **Target marker**: a transparent layered sight on the recommended enemy, or your own target when recommendations are off (**Sniper marker** in Battlegrounds settings). The skull is centered inside the ring. Under **Battlegrounds → Settings**, switch **Skull in target marker** or **Animate target marker** independently; `/hg sniper skull on|off` and `/hg sniper animate on|off` do the same. The marker gently pulses every 2.4 s by default, then pulses every 1.8 s at 50% or less, 1.2 s at 30% or less, and 0.8 s at 15% or less. An active kill window also uses the fastest pulse. Unknown health keeps the gentle pulse unless there is a kill-window cue; an immune or dead target does not pulse. A high recommendation score alone does not imply a finishing opportunity. There is no rotation or zoom, and Reduced motion keeps the marker steady. **Marker placement**: **Battlegrounds → Appearance → Sniper marker → Marker style** offers **New icon (default)** or **Legacy crosshair** (`/hg sniper style layered|legacy`); both share the pulse and placement controls. A mini preview beside the style dropdown shows the selected icon and skull setting. **Colours**: by default the ring takes the theme's colour: gold in Highground, Class Colors and Parchment, violet in Dark, copper in Ember and antique gold in Azeroth (`/hg sniper color theme`). The Ring / crosshair, Skull and Aiming mark / dot buttons open a colour picker with live previews; each has a Default button, and Cancel restores its previous colour. Custom colours persist during kill windows and save with profiles. Legacy supports separate crosshair and dot colours; its skull control is disabled. Commands: `/hg sniper color ring FF8800`, `/hg sniper color skull 33BB77`, `/hg sniper color aim default`. **Placement** offers Head area (approx., the default), Body (16 px lower), Left of nameplate, Right of nameplate and Above nameplate. Adjust the edge gap, horizontal/vertical offsets and size there. Head area is an estimate below the nameplate, not a helmet attachment: camera angle, model size and stacked nameplates can move it away from the head. Left/right/above use the nameplate frame's edges; other nameplate addons may need offset adjustments. Gap applies to those three edge placements and stays constant when resizing. Positive X moves right and positive Y moves up. Changing placement restores its default height while keeping gap, X and size. Custom placement heights survive the upgrade; untouched old body defaults move to head area. Commands: `/hg sniper place head`, `/hg sniper place left`, `/hg sniper place right`, `/hg sniper padding 12`, `/hg sniper x 0`, `/hg sniper y 0`. Use `/hg sniper test` on a target with a visible nameplate and `/hg go` for a kill-window preview. Named ring colours (`theme`, `gold`, `magenta` and others) and lock-on sound remain configurable with `/hg sniper`; a named colour or custom ring colour overrides the theme. Rings left on the old gold default now follow the theme. Fully restart WoW when first installing the textures; subsequent placement and animation code updates need only `/reload`.
- **Healer markers**: green crosses above friendly healers and on their Blizzard raid and party frames, and red crosses above known enemy healers (spec from the scoreboard, a remembered match, a talent-tree cast on Cata, a spec clue, or on WoW Forever a druid mostly out of cat and bear form). During a WoW Forever match the scoreboard shows only the class, so enemy markers there are rare. Toggle these in setup or **Battlegrounds → Settings → Targets**. Unknown roles and units without a nameplate show no marker.
- **Team healers**: a teammate is a healer when the game's group role says so (Retail), when Highground inspected their talents, or when you marked them. WoW Forever gives every teammate the damage role, so Highground inspects teammates whose class can heal before the gates open and marks those with the most points in a healing tree (**Inspect teammates for healers**, under Healer markers). To mark or unmark someone yourself, hover over them (nameplate, raid frame or character) and press your **Mark team healer** key (Key Bindings → AddOns), type `/hg healer` for the teammate under the mouse or your target, `/hg healer NAME`, or click their row in **Team healers** (`/hg healers`, or **Battlegrounds → Settings → Team healers**). Your mark beats an inspect and is kept for later matches on this client; marks aren't shared, because WoW Forever blocks addon messages during a match. `/hg healers test` shows sample teammates.
- **Team icons**: your group leader can give teammates an icon so you find them among strangers: a class icon, a role (**Tank**, **Healer**, **Melee**, **Caster**) or **Flag carrier**, **Escort**, **Defense**, **Offense**, **Caller**, **Peel**, **Scout**, **Focus** and **Squad A** to **D**. The icons mean nothing to Highground. They show above teammates' nameplates and on the left of Blizzard's raid and party frames; teammates with the same icon get their own outline colour. **Automatic icons** in the window gives everyone the leader hasn't marked their **Class** icon, or their **Role** from what your Highground knows (group role, team healers, a known spec, else a class whose damage specs are all melee or all ranged; mixed classes get none). Only the leader of your own group changes icons, and not in a battleground or arena, because the game blocks addon messages there and teammates wouldn't see the change. The leader's Highground sends them to group members with Highground before the match; members keep them in the match and after a `/reload`, and leaving the group drops them. The automatic rule also covers battleground teammates outside your group. When you lead a newly formed group, a small **quick panel** offers **Class icons**, **Role icons** and **Remove all**; **X** closes it, and so does entering a battleground or arena. It appears once per group: `/hg icons panel` or **Quick panel** in the Team icons window brings it back, and **Quick panel when you lead a group** (under **Team icons** in **Battlegrounds → Settings → Targets**) stops it appearing. The leader can also right-click a group member and choose **Team icon** (the roles at once, **Other** and **Class** submenus; not in a match or in combat), click a row in **Team icons** (`/hg icons`, or **Battlegrounds → Settings → Team icons**), hover over a teammate and press the **Set team icon** key (Key Bindings → AddOns), or type `/hg icon NAME ICON`: `none` hides someone's icon even under an automatic rule, `clear` gives back the automatic icon. Without a name it's the teammate under the mouse or your target; without an icon, the picker opens. `/hg icons auto off|class|role` sets the rule and `/hg icons clear` removes every icon and the rule. **Team icons** in **Battlegrounds → Settings → Targets** turns them off for you, with **Team icons on nameplates** and **Team icons on raid frames** under it. `/hg icons test` shows sample teammates.
- **Nameplates**: markers need the game's nameplates, so **Battlegrounds → Settings → Nameplates** sets them: **Nameplate range** (20 to 60 yards; the game may cap it lower), **Always show nameplates** (every unit in range, not only your target and units in combat) and **Friendly player nameplates** (needed for green healer markers; Shift+V also toggles them). These are the game's own settings: the game saves them, the controls show its current values, and they can't change in combat. `/hg nameplates` shows them; `/hg nameplates range 60`, `/hg nameplates all on|off` and `/hg nameplates friends on|off` change them.
- **Enemy burst markers** (Cata): while an enemy has a big offensive cooldown up, its icon sits beside their nameplate with **PURGE** if your class can remove it, otherwise **CC**. The **Burst markers** page sets each spell to Auto, Purge only, CC or Off.
- **Sap calls** (rogues, Warsong Gulch and Twin Peaks): when the enemy flag carrier or a known enemy healer dies, a raid warning says **SAP name at their graveyard**, with their resurrection countdown when known.

## Target recommendations

Turn off **Target recommendations** in Battlegrounds settings (or `/hg priority off`) to pick your own targets. This removes the enemy panel and card, the target, focus and mark keys, the GO sound and the map's kill-target mark. The target marker stays and follows your own target.

**Target behavior** sets:

- **Switch margin** (default 8 points): how far ahead another enemy must be before the recommendation switches or rows reorder. A GO candidate must also be within it.
- **GO alert** (default 12 seconds after a defensive ends; 0 turns GO off) and the **defensive-spent** bonus, which fades over 60 seconds.
- **Reorder enemy rows by priority** (off by default): move rows to match rank outside combat. Turning it off keeps their current order.
- **Use live scores for priority ranks and sorting** (off by default): include current combat signals in the rank, rather than base priority alone. The attack recommendation always considers live signals, even with both sorting options off.
- The **execute threshold** (default 35%): enemy rows show a red execute outline at or below it, and on Cata the health text turns red.
- On Cata, **Preserve breakable crowd control** (on by default).

Known full immunities never win, whatever the points. Retail reads only clear aura categories and does not guess immunity or breakable CC.

The other targeting pages set point values: **Target priority**, **Objectives**, **Live signals**, **Cooldowns**, **Spec ratings** and **Advanced multipliers**. On **Live signals**, **Targeting one of your healers** (10 points) lifts an enemy in view that targets one of your team healers, so the recommendation helps you peel. Hover a value to see its range; multipliers show the effective value (points × your multiplier × battleground modifier, for example a flag bonus of 30 × 1 × 1.5 = 45 in capture-the-flag maps). On **Cooldowns**, **Show spell overrides** lists per-spell values: leave a field blank to use the general value, type a number to fix it, press **x** to clear it.

Spec ratings (0–15) are starting points, not measured odds. Only Retail Frost Death Knight has hand-written matchups; other specs use generic armor, protection and mobility modifiers.

**Reset target tuning** restores points, multipliers, spec ratings and target behavior. It keeps feature switches, appearance and cooldown tracking.

### Cooldown tracking (Cata)

The main panel tracks big defensives, offensive cooldowns, and long crowd control or silences (Blind, Smoke Bomb, Hammer of Justice, Strangulate). The arena panel separately tracks observed interrupts and diminishing returns. The **Cooldown tracking** page turns single spells on or off; search by spell, class or type (`offensive`, `defensive`) and use **Track shown** / **Untrack shown**. An untracked spell loses its main-panel display, score bonus and spent alert; full immunities still block recommendations. Choices survive reloads and **Reset target tuning**, and are included in full profiles.

## Profiles

Every character keeps its own settings for each addon (Battlegrounds with all its sizes, Arena, Nearby, Bounties, Macros and General), and they save as you change them; there is nothing to load or switch. A character you play for the first time starts as a copy of the last one you played. **Profiles** (`/hg profile [ADDON]`) picks an addon and lists your other characters and imported profiles. **Copy into [character]** (click twice; `/hg profile copy ADDON NAME`) replaces this character's settings for that addon; for Battlegrounds, every size. **Delete** removes another character's copy or an import (`/hg profile delete NAME`).

**Export** (`/hg export [ADDON]`) shows this character's settings for the chosen addon as a code. **Import** (`/hg import [CODE]`) keeps a pasted code in the list under the character name it was exported from, such as **Jaina**, or **Jaina (2)** when that name is taken; nothing changes until you copy it. **Share in chat** (`/hg profile share [ADDON]`) puts a link in your chat box; a friend who clicks it gets the profile the same way. Both players need Highground and must be online and able to whisper each other; links last 30 minutes or until the sender reloads. Codes from older versions still import and offer every addon. Profiles saved by older versions became imports with their names; the built-in Default and Automatic switching are gone. Macros, keybindings and records are never part of profiles.

## Appearance

Open an addon's **Appearance** page from its sidebar or a panel cogwheel. Choose a sample and **Start preview** to see changes as you edit. Use **Stop preview** or `/hg test stop` to finish. Leaving the page, combat or matches also ends it. Samples preserve saved panel positions and do not enable features or create real records. Appearance edits save automatically. **Shared theme and accessibility** opens Shared appearance, whose preview selector covers your enabled addons.

- **Battlegrounds → Appearance**: **Enemy style** (row preset, health style, bar texture and colour, contrast); target marker style, colours, size and placement; enemy count; **Overall battleground scale**, which multiplies one scale per battleground panel (main panel, **Resurrection timers**, **Timers**, **Win forecast**, **Flag carriers** and **Quick callouts**); sizes and spacing, including **Bar thickness** (Bar style) and **Thin line height** (Thin line style); **Enemy rows** switches, starting with **Hero card**. Enemy **Bar texture** and **Health bar color** can follow **Shared styling** or override it for this panel and its suggested-target card. Choose class, faction, green to red (by health left), **Theme default** (the active accent), or **Custom color** with its colour picker. Shared appearance remains the default for the other panels. For **Readable cards**, contrast controls and command examples, see [Enemy appearance](enemy-appearance.md).
- **Arena → Appearance**: Arena panel scale, compact spacing, width, row spacing, padding and header height.
- **Nearby → Appearance**: row count, scale, compact spacing, width, row spacing, padding and header height.
- **Bounties → Appearance**: nameplate markers and wanted-poster alerts.
- **Shared preferences → Shared appearance**: theme, font, text size, Reduced motion, panel transparency, background opacity, Frameless, bar textures and colours, and borders.

Detailed sizes, row elements and shared styling are shown directly on their **Appearance** pages; scroll to reach the rest. Normal and compact layouts keep their own dimensions. Arena and Nearby no longer follow Battlegrounds scale or spacing changes; existing installations and imported profiles keep their previous effective layouts. Panel widths still expand as needed to fit text. **Reset panel styling** affects that addon’s scales, dimensions and row styling. **Reset shared fonts and styling** affects the shared font, text size, bars, borders and Frameless settings. Both keep the selected theme and Reduced motion.

**Highground** is the default theme; **Class Colors**, **Dark**, **Ember**, **Parchment** and **Azeroth** are also available. Theme changes apply immediately, including in combat, without moving panels. **Reduced motion** replaces animated indicators with steady ones. **Panel transparency** is on by default: HUD backgrounds use 65% of the chosen opacity while idle and brighten on hover or while their cogwheel menu is open. Text, icons and timers keep their visibility. Compact battleground columns brighten together. **Frameless** hides the background and border, and hides locked title bars.

**Shared preferences → General → All HUD panels** controls master scale (50–200%, default 80%). It multiplies each panel’s own scale. Size and layout changes made in combat apply when it ends; background opacity and transparency apply immediately.

**Compact layout** in **Battlegrounds → Settings** makes enemy rows and the suggested-target card one line and docks carrier, resurrection, win forecast and Timers panels below the enemy panel. Drag any docked panel to move the column. Arena and Nearby keep their own positions and compact preferences. **Hide quest tracker** (advanced, same page) hides Blizzard's quest tracker and Questie's during battlegrounds and brings them back afterwards. In combat they only fade until combat ends.

## Battleground panels

The resurrection and **Timers** panels show countdowns over thin progress bars: Timers lists captures, flag respawns and other objective countdowns. Amber marks the last five seconds, `~` an estimate and `--` unknown. A faction crest shows which team a graveyard, base or capture belongs to; **YOU** marks your own resurrection destination. Hover for details. The **flag carrier panel** puts each team's faction crest on its carrier's class icon; while no carrier is seen, the row shows the dimmed crest. **No carrier seen** means no carrier has been observed; **Flag dropped** describes an observed drop. Use the panel to protect your carrier or stop theirs: left-click to target, right-click to focus. The optional low-health resurrection reminder reports timing without telling you to die. See [observations and timers](observations.md) for how positions and countdowns are worked out.

The **Win forecast** panel appears in Arathi Basin, Eye of the Storm, Battle for Gilneas, Deepwind Gorge and Darkspear Islands once a winner can be predicted. It names the winner and the time left if the score continues and captures in progress complete; its underline is the winner's score and its header the live score. The line below says how many bases the trailing team must hold to win instead. **If defended** appears when retaking the contested bases would change the winner or the time by more than five seconds. Turn it off with **Battlegrounds → Settings → Win forecast**; flag and epic battlegrounds never show it.

Shift-click the win forecast or a resurrection or Timers countdown to share it in chat, for example "Horde res at Stables in 12s" or "Vexa (Horde) is capping Gold Mine: 3.2s". Only what Highground works out is shared: the score, base owners, flag carriers and the game's own announcements are already on everyone's screen. The line goes into your chat box on the callout channel (`/hg call s|i|raid|party|y`, or **Help and commands → Choose call channel**; say by default), or into the message you are typing. Press Enter to send it: the game blocks chat sent by addons during a match, but not yours. Previews and the guide print the sample instead.

**Objective overview** (optional, in **Battlegrounds → Settings**) adds ownership of graveyard bases and enemy sightings from the last 30 seconds to the Timers panel. A base being captured appears once, as its capture row with the countdown and any recent sightings. Sightings show how many enemies were seen and how long ago; a base with none shows only its name. Friendly bases close to falling receive an urgent warning. Low-health resurrection reminders are suppressed while you carry a flag or orb. On cart, Strand and epic maps, the Timers panel also shows observed objective widgets, map landmarks and recent announcements. It can display cart control, gate state, round text and reinforcements when the game exposes them. It does not invent delivery, gate or boss countdowns when the client supplies none.

### Localized announcements

Flag, orb and node messages recognize recorded formats for English, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese clients. Live node names identify the local objective. Unknown formats keep the existing API and map observations; custom server wording and locales without recorded formats still need in-client verification.

### Quick callouts

Enable **Battlegrounds → Settings → Quick callout panel** for a separate movable panel. It starts off, including on upgrades. In named battleground areas, it shows your current location and **Help**, **1**, **2**, **3+** and **All clear** buttons. Each click immediately sends a message to battleground chat, such as `Need help — Farm (3+ enemies)`. **Help** omits the count; **All clear** sends `All clear — Farm` to say everything is okay there. The buttons are game macros (`/i …`), so a click sends like your own macro, also during matches where the game blocks addon chat; a message you are typing stays intact. The game does not let addons change these buttons in combat: until combat ends, the panel keeps the area and destination it had and shows **Updates after combat** when one is waiting.

Two additional switches under **Quick callout panel** start off, including on upgrades:

- **Enemy destination callouts:** choose a destination under **Enemies going to**, then click **1**, **2** or **3+**. For example, while at Lumber Mill, choose Farm and click **2** to send `2 enemies going to Farm`, or **All clear** to send `All clear — Farm` once it is safe again. Selecting a destination does not send a message or change the local Help buttons. The selection resets between matches. Base destinations are available in Arathi Basin, Battle for Gilneas, Eye of the Storm, Deepwind Gorge and Darkspear Islands (WoW Forever); other locations come from the current map's landmarks, with localized names where available.
- **EFC route callouts:** click **Ramp**, **Graveyard** or **Tunnel** in Warsong Gulch, or **Ramp**, **Graveyard** or **Water** in Twin Peaks, to send messages such as `EFC going tunnel`. These are manual sightings; no detected carrier or open flag panel is required.

Drag the panel header while panels are unlocked; its position is saved per character. Like the main panel, it can't move in combat: a position reset waits until combat ends. It stays separate in Compact layout. **Arrange panels** previews it outside a match with sending disabled. Local help follows your named area; enabled destination and EFC controls also work on roads and in midfield. The panel hides outside battlegrounds and works with the timers switched off. All its send buttons share the same cooldown. Disable **Quick callout panel** to hide it and its extra controls.

### Match history

`/hg history` keeps each character's last 100 observed PvP matches. Search by map, result, character or specialization. Rows show the map, result, duration and date, with your spec icon when known. The selected match shows a card with the result, your specialization, when you joined and how long it lasted; tiles for the personal scoreboard statistics the game reported (rating change only when it moved); and the latest 11 of its observed objective events, up to 1,000 per match. **Full timeline** opens all of them for copying. Timelines live in the bundled **HighgroundMatchData** addon, which loads only when you look at an older match, or after a battleground once 20 newer timelines wait; without it, Highground keeps up to 200 events per match and the window says when an older timeline can't be shown. Leaving before an observed result is recorded as **Left early**, as is a match that ends while you are disconnected; a `/reload` or a reconnect during the match continues the same entry. Unavailable stats remain absent. The displayed win rate counts recorded wins and losses. **Undo delete** restores the most recently deleted entry while the window is available.

## Arena panel

The panel handles arena matches on Retail and Cataclysm Classic; duels do not open it. The Arena addon is off by default: turn on **Enable addon** in **Arena → Overview and enable** (or the **Addons** page) and reload. Then use **Arena → Settings** to show the panel, **Appearance** to size it, and **Previews and guide** to try opponents or move the panel. WoW Forever has no arenas, so Highground there has no Arena pages, commands or guide.

Rows use a large class/spec icon, name, solid health bar, PvP trinket, casts and **TGT**/**FOC** marks, without redundant class/spec text. Crowd control temporarily replaces the icon. Specs are the ones the arena announces before the gates. In arena matches, fixed **A1–A3** labels on Retail and up to **A1–A5** on Cata use matching arena unit bindings for combat clicks. Without announced specs before the gates, the panel shows a row for each member of your team's size, so an opponent revealed in combat still has one. Unseen opponents keep their name and spec; each Solo Shuffle round starts with the new opponents. Trinkets show client-reported timing only: **?** means unavailable, not ready. Retail sends restricted data directly to display widgets; Cata adds diminishing-return strength (**50%**, **25%**, **immune**), a conservative 20-second reset after overlapping control ends, and observed interrupt estimates. Talents and resets can shorten those cooldowns.

## Nearby enemies

Outside battlegrounds, arenas, dungeons and raids, **Nearby enemies** lists hostile players you have seen, newest first: a large class icon, name, level and sighting age. Hiding spells or an earlier location supply context; full location stays in hover details. Hover for guild and encounter history. The panel starts on in new installations; toggle it in **Nearby → Settings** or with `/hg nearby on|off`. Existing saved choices are kept. Left-click targets, right-click focuses and returns to your previous target. Sources are nameplates, your target, focus and mouseover; on Cata the combat log adds enemies without a visible nameplate. **Nearby → Settings** controls fading, forgetting and sounds; **Nearby → Appearance** controls row count, scale, compact spacing and dimensions. Enemies first seen in combat show as **+N after combat** in the header until combat ends. The **NEARBY: N** header counts distinct visible, living enemy players across nameplates, target, focus and mouseover. The list and count include visible opposing-faction players on flight paths even when they cannot be attacked. It updates on sightings, nameplate departures and once a second, including during combat, and includes enemies beyond the row limit. The counter stays visible at **0** while enabled in the open world. Remembered rows and combat-log-only sightings do not increase it; those rows keep their normal fade and forget times. Target, focus and mouseover sightings stay fresh while the enemy remains visible. Clearing recent sightings leaves the live counter running.

**Fight mode:** in a party or raid in the open world, with **Use Highground in open-world fights** on (**Battlegrounds → Settings → Open world**), this list becomes your kill order once an enemy is seen in your zone. Rows follow the kill-order rank, shown on a tile over the class icon; the kill target's row and tile are highlighted, health runs along the bottom of each row and replaces the sighting age while the enemy is in view, and an eye marks enemies targeting you. Rows reorder out of combat; in combat they keep their enemies and keep updating. With **Target recommendations** off, the list keeps its usual order without ranks. The battleground enemy board no longer opens in the open world, so this is the only list. Healer crosses, burst marks and the target crosshair still follow the fight.

New enemies play a soft two-note chime, limited to one sighting sound per ten seconds. **Sound: on/off** at the bottom of the panel is the Nearby enemy **Sound** switch from Nearby's alert settings; **Sounds** in Shared preferences and per-enemy mutes still apply. Stealth cues follow the **Enemy stealth** switch. When a nearby enemy uses Stealth, Prowl, Vanish, Invisibility, Shadowmeld or Camouflage, chat says so, and again when it fades. Distinct lower- and higher-pitched stealth cues accompany entry and exit. On Cata the combat log confirms both; on Retail and WoW Forever readable casts or hiding auras on visible enemies reveal entry, and visible reappearance without a hiding aura indicates exit. Unreadable game data cannot confirm a transition.

History belongs to the current character, faction and realm or Forever ruleset. It remembers every enemy that character meets: how many times, how often they killed you and you them. Encounter totals and kills stay in the hover details and journal; an open profile updates when a kill or death is recorded. Routine sighting messages follow your alert level. These are remembered sightings, not proof that someone is still nearby. Cata counts your killing blows from the combat log. Retail and WoW Forever count your and your pet's killing blows on an enemy in your sightings or saved history, and honorable-kill messages that name one; a killing blow and its honor message count once. A missing realm is matched only when the name identifies one enemy. WoW Forever names are unique, so it ignores a realm in the message. Generic honor gains and ambiguous names add no kill. On those clients, a death counts when the game's death recap names the enemy. `/hg nearby forget` clears only that character’s current history, except tracked enemies and bounty targets. Older records without an identifiable owner and ruleset stay preserved in SavedVariables but are hidden. See [data scopes](data-scopes.md) for the boundaries used throughout the addon.

**Shared sightings** send the enemies you see, with where you stood, to your party or raid every 3 seconds and your guild every 10, and list theirs from your zone as **Seen by NAME** rows (**(guild)** for guild members). Someone else's sighting makes no sound and adds no history; once you see that enemy yourself, the row is yours again. An enemy still in sight goes out again after 15 seconds (group) or 30 (guild), or once you move, and not at all when a group member already reported it. Only the open world shares: instances stop it both ways, and chat lockdown holds batches. **World map sightings** pins your own and shared sightings on the world, zone (Shift+M) and continent maps where their observer stood, with a circle for how far away a recent one could be (melee, about 28 yards or a nameplate's 60). Close enemies share one pin with a count; hover for who saw them and when. Pins fade with **Fade** and go when Nearby forgets the enemy. **Nearby → Settings** has **World map sightings**, **Share sightings with your group** and **Also share with your guild**, all on by default, also `/hg nearby map|share|share guild on|off`. Guild members anywhere receive where you stood. **Share sightings with every Highground user** (`/hg nearby share world on|off`, on by default, not on Cataclysm) sends a map square about 5% of the zone wide to the realm/faction users channel every 20 seconds instead of your spot, and lists everyone's sightings from your own zone as **Reported by NAME**; other zones' only add heat. **Enemy heatmap** (`/hg nearby heat on|off`, on, under **World map sightings**) shades the world, zone and continent maps where enemies were seen and who won the fights there in the last hour: yours, your group's and guild's, and everyone's from any zone. Each enemy counts once, at its latest spot; newer data counts more and nothing older than an hour counts. Turning it or **World map sightings** off discards what it gathered. Squares are about 100 yards on a zone map and get finer as you zoom into a continent or the world map; each sits where its sightings are and is as wide as how far off they can be (a nameplate's reach, or everyone's coarse squares). A square is the enemy faction's colour (Horde red, Alliance blue), or yours where your side killed more, strongest where an enemy raid (**Enemy raid**, 8) gathered within about 150 yards, and fades out over the hour. A square with kills gets a badge: the crest of the side ahead (crossed swords when even) and both sides' kills, yours first (`5–2`). A square without one means enemies were seen there but no kills. Hover a square for the count, both sides' kills, who is winning and when it was last seen. **Layers** (WoW Forever): players on another layer of a zone are invisible to you, so the list, pins and heat leave out sightings from another layer: a group member's in your zone whom the game marks as on another layer, and yours from before the game moved you. Guild and everyone's sightings have an unknown layer and stay. Rows tag another layer, map and row tooltips name the layer, and tracking alerts add **on another layer**. The world map's **Layers** button, **Also show enemies seen on other layers** in Nearby → Settings and `/hg nearby layers all|mine` show them greyed; the button's tooltip counts each kind. Map tooltips and World activity kills show a bounty's reward. `/hg nearby` reports sharing; `/hg nearby test` adds a guild sample row and pins the samples, sample marks, heat and fight badges (your side ahead, the enemy ahead, an even fight) around you: on your zone map, its continent and the world map, placed like real sightings. Without a zone map they show on the map you are viewing. **Kill order in world fights** (on, `/hg nearby fights on|off`, needs the Battlegrounds module): in a party or raid in the open world, the battleground kill order, health bars, CC and **on you** signals, healer markers and burst marks run on the enemies you saw and the ones your group or guild shared from your zone in the last 30 seconds. It starts when an enemy is seen in your zone and ends a minute after the last one, never during combat; the panel can't appear in combat, so a fight that starts in combat shows it afterwards. Battleground-only parts (timers, flags, objectives, map positions, spec tags, capture sharing) stay off. **Tracked enemies**: select an enemy in the journal and click **Track** (also `/hg nearby track NAME` or `target`, `/hg nearby untrack NAME`; at most 20). When you, your group or your guild see one, the **Tracked enemy** alert says where, with a sound and a centre-screen message (its switches are in Nearby's alert settings), at most every two minutes per zone; muted enemies stay quiet. Tracked enemies are listed from any zone as **Tracked** rows and get their own marked, named pin on the map. The journal shows the latest report; `/hg nearby tracked` lists them. **Watch for tracked enemies with every Highground user** (Nearby → Settings, `/hg nearby watch on|off`, on by default, not on Cataclysm) asks Highground users of your faction on this realm to watch for yours and reports theirs: a report gives the zone, sub-zone and a map square about 5% wide, not where the reporter stood. Everyone in the channel sees whom you track. **Tracked enemy alerts** (`/hg nearby tracking on|off`) turns tracking alerts off. **Map marks**: Alt+right-click a zone map and choose **Enemies here**, **Ganker** or **Help needed**, or use `/hg nearby mark enemies|ganker|help` or the **Mark enemies at my position** key for where you stand. Marks go to your group and guild like sightings, show on the map (close marks of one type merge with a count) and end after 10, 15 and 5 minutes; yours are sent again every two minutes for players who join later. **Clear my marks** in that menu, or `/hg nearby mark clear`, removes them for everyone. **Share map marks with every Highground user** is opt-in (`/hg nearby marks world on|off`) and shows them where you marked. **World PvP calls** (`/hg nearby calls on|off`) alert you in your zone when your group or guild sees an enemy who killed you at least **Known ganker** times (3), when at least **Enemy raid** enemies (8) were seen close together in the last minute, and when someone places a mark; each repeats at most every five minutes. Marks switch off with `/hg nearby marks off`. The **Map marks** switch remains available when **World map sightings** is off: hiding your map pins does not stop sharing marks or receiving their calls. **World activity** (`/hg activity`, or **Open world activity** on Nearby's overview) lists the zones where you, your group or your guild recently saw enemies, marked the map or traded kills, most recent first: select one for its enemy count, classes, guilds, marks and the last kills, then **Show on map**. `/hg activity test`, or **World activity** on Nearby's Previews page, shows sample zones. **Share kills and deaths** (`/hg nearby feed on|off`, on) sends your kills of enemy players and your deaths to them, with where you stood, to the same group and guild and lists theirs there for 30 minutes; with sharing with everyone, a coarse square goes to every Highground user too, and theirs add only to the heatmap.

Click **History** at the bottom of the nearby panel, even when the live list is empty, or open `/hg journal` to search enemy encounters by name, class, guild, area or note. **Previous/Next** browses up to 1,000 saved enemies across sessions, most recently seen first; when the journal is full, the longest unseen enemy makes room. **All**, **Tracked** and **Bounty** beside the search show every enemy, only tracked enemies or only enemies with a bounty (also `/hg journal tracked|bounty|all`). Tracked enemies and bounty targets are never deleted: not to make room, not by `/hg nearby forget` and not by **Delete entry**, which says to stop tracking first or that the bounty must end. Add a personal note, mute that player's encounter alerts, start a bounty or delete an entry. Muting keeps the player's history and suppresses nearby, stealth and bounty sighting alerts for them. Search has a **Clear search** action; paging selects a visible entry. **Undo delete** restores the last removed record without replacing a newer encounter with that player.

## Bounties

Bounties is in alpha, marked by an **Alpha** badge: it can still change and has more bugs. Please report problems on the [Highground Discord](https://discord.gg/xu3VsxRjca).

**Bounties → Settings** controls sounds, telling your group (off by default), sharing sightings, the re-alert delay, the fallback officer rank, expiry, alert channels and **Also alert for bounty targets you can't attack**; its **Bounties** switch turns the feature off. **Bounties → Appearance** controls wanted posters and the coin and row markers. **Previews and guide** offers every sample scenario and the posting and hunting guides.

`/hg bounty test` toggles a wanted poster and sample Nearby row, with the same placement, dragging, hover pause, ten-second expiry and alert queue as live sightings. Use `test poster`, `test match`, `test kill` or `test board` to open those UIs directly; `create`, `gold`, `guild`, `pot`, `exchange`, `claims` and `suggestions` open their sample views. The test board starts with three distinct targets per audience, varied rewards, shared contributions and a pending guild submission. Editing, tracking, added gold and claims stay with the selected sample target. There is no tour window. `test stop` closes the session. Sample actions leave real rewards, settings, claims and drafts unchanged; they send no messages and take no screenshots. Dragging a test poster saves its position for future live alerts. Start previews out of combat; entering combat closes the sample session.

`/hg bounty guide` opens an interactive **Bounty feature tour**. Choose any chapter in the left menu or follow **Previous / Next feature**; the counter shows which chapters you have visited. It covers nearby alerts and markers, wanted cards and full portraits, creation and kill/expiry terms, account gold and the bag tooltip, guild approval, shared arrivals and filters, adding gold to a pot, sharing/importing, killing blows and screenshot states, claims/payments, and death-report suggestions. Examples stay open while you explore them. Try the sample forms and switches, **Start tracking / Ignore**, repeated kills, screenshot failure/retry, and approving, rejecting or marking sample claims paid. They use separate sample windows: your real drafts, gold, settings, bounties and claims are unchanged. No screenshot is taken and no message is sent to other players. Copyable sample posts contain a tour-only code that cannot import into your real board. The portrait uses your selected player or your own character, labelled as a sample. Visuals work even with live alerts disabled; sound follows your settings. Jump directly with `/hg bounty guide nearby`, `board`, `create`, `gold`, `guild`, `shared` (or `world`), `pot`, `exchange`, `kill`, `claims`, or `suggestions`. **Return** or Escape in a sample dialog brings you back; **Close tour**, **Finish tour**, or `/hg bounty guide stop` closes every sample. Running the command again while a sample is open also closes the tour. When launched from the Addons directory, closing it returns to that guide.

Put a bounty on an enemy player, with or without gold. When the target shows up in the open world, a battleground or an arena, Highground plays a sound, shows a wanted poster and puts a gold coin beside their nameplate. Their Highground rows get a gold frame and their tooltip names the reward. Highground keeps the list; players pay the gold themselves.

The compact parchment popup shows the target, who posted the reward, the reward and the sighting location without an internal scrollbar. **View bounty** keeps full notes, reward terms and contributions in a compact summary, with one target header for multiple contributions. A visible player supplies their actual character portrait, retained locally so **View bounty** can still show it after they disappear. Their observed race and body type are saved even when the client cannot provide a creature model ID, as can happen with player models on WoW Forever. These details and any available model ID are included in guild sync and shared-bounty messages. After a reload, or on a recipient who has never seen the target, the portrait tooltip’s **RACE PORTRAIT** label identifies generic race artwork and **MODEL LIKENESS** identifies a reconstruction from a saved model. Neither reproduces the player's full face customization, hair or gear; seeing the player replaces it with the actual portrait. With no usable saved likeness, the card uses a class icon, or a coin if the class is unknown. WoW does not expose the rendered face as a portable image, and a player GUID alone cannot recreate it. Targets observed before race data was saved need another sighting to acquire this fallback.

Posters close after ten seconds; hover to pause, drag to save a new position, dismiss with **×**, or choose **View bounty**. Simultaneous sightings queue up to three more posters; queued sightings older than thirty seconds are discarded. The existing re-alert delay, journal mutes, shared reward minimum and alert filters still apply. Portrait observations use the existing guild-sharing and public publishing/receiving preferences. A poster records a sighting, not proof the enemy remains nearby.

**Bounty details** uses the same parchment wanted cards, with the reward shown once per contribution. Shared bounties show each contributor's note, funding and expiry on their own card, plus a labelled **Total pledged** when there is more than one contribution. Cards stack at larger text sizes; scroll to reach every contribution. Portraits update when the matching character becomes available, and a captured likeness stays on that character's card while browsing. Each card lists its terms as labelled rows: **Progress**, **Posted by**, **Funds**, **Expires**, **Finish**, sightings and **Last kill**. **Last seen** gives the latest report's place, reporter and age. **First seen** appears when the first report differs and keeps its area, zone, reporter and time through reloads, sync and exports. **You saw** marks your own earlier observation after another hunter reported. Dates use your display language and the game's 12/24-hour clock setting: **Today, 18:40**, **Wed, 6:40 PM**, **2 Dec, 18:40**. Tracking automatically shares visible open-world sightings with the bounty's audience; guild reports follow the guild-sharing preference and private sightings stay local. Recent reports in your zone rise to the top of the board and show a **Reported in your zone** poster; distant trackers get a chat update. Same-zone priority lasts two minutes and does not imply a measured distance. Existing alert filters, ignores and mutes apply; reports are throttled and never automatically forwarded. Unseen named targets have no invented origin. The editor, settings and **Claims & payments** share the board window. **Back/Forward** preserve drafts and filters; **Back to board** discards the current draft. Saving returns to My bounties.

Open the **bounty board** with `/hg bounty`, its key binding, **Bounties → Settings**, or a player's right-click menu (Retail and WoW Forever; not in battlegrounds, arenas, combat or other restricted play). An empty personal board offers **Create first bounty**. Choose the **Shared board** tab to hunt for other players. **Help** brings the guide back without losing your search. Start with **Only me** and no reward if you simply want alerts for a player.

The three main views are **My bounties**, **Guild & communities** and **Shared board**. Select a guild or native WoW character community in the middle view; each has its own board, including communities with identical names. Each shows one parchment wanted card per exact target, with its portrait, reward and audience: **Only me**, **Guild**, the selected community, **Shared**, or a linked organization/shared audience. Click a card or **View bounty** to open the full wanted poster popup; closing it restores the same board and scroll position. My bounties shows your targets, tracked rewards and rewards you posted; the shared total includes other contributors. The guild/community view has an **Awaiting approval** filter for your submissions and, for moderators, the review queue. Search finds players, realms, posters and audiences. **Import bounty** stays visible on the board. The visible **Suggestions** tab offers optional targets from death reports; settings stay under **Settings**.

**Create bounty** has three steps: **Target → Reward → Review**. Choose the player and audience, then enter gold per kill (`1` or `1g`), shared expiry and an optional note. One kill and any finish are the defaults; **Customize hunt** exposes additional conditions. The review shows the target, audience, terms and full commitment. Nothing is posted before the final confirmation. **Back** keeps the draft; **Cancel** on the first step discards it. Shared rewards need at least 1g; private tracking can be free. Organization submissions require membership. **Use target** accepts another player, including friendly or unflagged players; an invalid target leaves the draft intact.

- **Only me → Save private bounty** saves a private target. Gold is optional.
- **Guild / community → Submit bounty** submits to the selected guild or community. Gold is optional. Guild officers and community owners, leaders and moderators approve submissions; ordinary members submit for review.
- **Shared → Enable sharing & publish** opts into publishing and creates the contribution in one explicit action. Once enabled, the action says **Publish shared bounty**. Invalid drafts do not change the opt-in, and publishing does not automatically enable receiving.
- Cards show **TRACKING** or **NOT TRACKING**. **Start tracking / Stop tracking** changes sighting alerts without pledging gold or deleting rewards. **View report**, **Review report** and **Record payment** appear when a report needs attention. Open **View bounty** for visible **Share bounty**, **Edit reward** or **Claim reward** actions. Your own rewards use **Edit reward**; other players can contribute with **Add gold**. **Manage bounty** lists audience changes, tracking and removal directly. There are no card or poster “More” menus.
- **View bounty** opens the selected target's wanted poster popup: reward, audience, every contribution in this view, funding, expiry and notes. There is no second list to select from. **Share bounty** for private and group rewards also offers **Share in chat**, which prepares a message for you to send. Recipients review it before importing a private copy; an existing private bounty is replaced only after confirmation. `/hg bounty share NAME` prepares the same link.
- **Settings → Shared alert settings** controls shared browsing, advertising and the notification minimum. **Claims & payments** opens kill reports and payment records. **Refresh** requests updates from online participants and appears when that board can receive updates.
- Guild and community cards show whether moderator approval is pending or complete. This status applies to that listing; a linked shared contribution can already be published. Moderators can reject or remove a listing; submitters can remove their own.
- Officers are the guild master and the ranks with the officer checkbox in the guild's rank permissions. If the game doesn't provide rank permissions, the ranks up to **Officers** in Bounties settings count instead (default 1, the rank below the guild master).
- Guild and community bounties expire after 14 days by default. Online members exchange updates. Native community membership and roles are checked again when sending or receiving updates; communities can span realms without enabling global sharing. Clients without native communities offer the guild board.
- Open-world sightings of guild bounty targets are shared with the guild. On Cata, your killing blow on one tells the guild.
- WoW Forever first-and-last names work as typed (`Iron Fist`).

**Kill goals and preferred finishes:** new bounties default to one kill and any finish. **Customize hunt** exposes the 1–20 kill goal and optional preferred finishes; editing custom terms expands them automatically. Gold is per approved kill, and the form shows the full commitment. Preferred finishes need evidence of the setup and death; notes and bonuses are reviewed separately. Published shared kill counts and preferences cannot be edited; withdraw and create a new contribution to change them.

**Kill detection and screenshots:** readable combat-log killing blows are confirmed locally; named honor credit on restricted clients may include assists and stays labelled **HONOR CREDIT**. Detected credit automatically reports all eligible rewards for the exact target, including imported private rewards. **View report** opens that death’s evidence and reward status without creating another claim. If detection missed a kill, use **Claim reward**, or **Report a missed kill** for another death. Reward approval and payment remain separate. The screenshot checkmark sits beside its explicit status and appears only after the game confirms saving; failures offer **Retry screenshot**. Images stay in `WoW/Screenshots`. **Copy proof (optional)** prepares text for sharing; only global proof offers Discord destinations.

**Exporting and importing:** **View bounty → Share bounty**, or `/hg bounty export NAME`, produces a bounty link labelled `PRIVATE`, `GUILD`, `COMMUNITY` or `GLOBAL`. Paste the bounty link, complete shared post or older code into the board’s **Import bounty** button, or `/hg bounty import`, review its audience and terms, then confirm. Existing private entries require replacement confirmation. Guild and community exports import as private drafts and need separate submission and moderator approval before publication. Community exports copy the selected reward and grant no native membership or approval. Private, guild and community exports have no global Discord posting flow. Short in-game chat links also identify their source audience; older links still import as private copies.

**Global bounties on Discord:** publishing returns to the board. Open **View bounty → Share bounty** for optional sharing. One bounty link is selected by default: press Ctrl+C, send it, and have the recipient choose **Import bounty → Review import**. **Select link to copy** reselects that same link. **Discord channel** opens a separate dialog with the destination address. Private and group rewards offer **Share in chat**. Older codes and complete posts still work for import. The link and raw code contain the same bounty snapshot; the link is for addon import, not a browser. Discord posting is optional because the reward is already published in game. The [Highground Discord](https://discord.gg/xu3VsxRjca) has matching `#bounties-pve`, `#bounties-pvp`, `#bounties-rp` and `#bounties-rp-pvp` boards. Hunters can import while the creator is offline; this enables receiving, not publishing, and preserves the creator, expiry and terms. Imported funding and identity remain unverified until the posting character sends an authenticated update. Imports cannot overwrite authenticated records, extend an expiry, grant review authority or cross game/region/realmset/faction boundaries. Explicit imports remain eligible for sighting alerts at their stated amount; network offers use funded totals. Repost an updated link when the bounty changes. **Updated bounty links:** **Select link to copy**, then Ctrl+C, copies a `highground://bounty/` link to paste in **Import bounty**. The open export refreshes when the pot changes. Copy and repost the updated link or complete post to Discord; previously pasted links remain snapshots. Pot exports use `HGB3` and keep original reviewers, contributions and terms. Older `HGB2` exports remain importable. Imports can refresh earlier unverified snapshots, while stale links cannot roll back authenticated records. Private and guild exports retain their audience, and global exports omit private rewards. These are addon import links, not hosted web pages. Global killed posters link to the matching `#bounty-proofs-*` channel. Each channel has a pinned template for the original bounty post, contribution ID, target/hunter, region and ruleset, kill number, time, location, preferred finish and attached screenshot/video. The addon does not upload or automatically post to Discord.

**New bounty matches:** incoming funded shared rewards and approved guild/community rewards that meet your minimum show a **NEW BOUNTY · FILTER MATCH** poster. It names who posted the reward (the largest pledge, and how many others pledged) and where the target was last seen, or says it has not been spotted. **Start tracking** adds the target to My bounties without pledging gold. **Ignore bounty** silences arrival and sighting alerts for that target; use **Start tracking** on its card to resume. Closing the poster keeps the board entry. Choices survive reloads and remain separate between Forever realmsets. Notification preferences still apply.

**Account gold** tracking starts **on**. **Gold & settings** in the creation form, **Settings → Account gold & funding**, or `/hg bounty gold` shows each character's gold, the total, existing commitments and the amount available for a new reward. Opening it from an editor preserves the draft; **Return** takes you straight back to it. Hover the gold in your bags for the same breakdown. Log into each character once to record its balance; the current character updates when money changes, while alts show their last recorded balance. Characters share a gold pool only within the same server, or the same active realmset in WoW Forever. The ledger is stored in HighgroundBountyData, local to your WoW account and installation. It never sends character balances to other players. Account-bank and guild-bank gold are not included. **Require enough gold before creating a bounty** starts **off**. The reward field warns when the available amount is too low, and you can still create the bounty. Turn the setting on in **Gold & settings** to block rewards that exceed the available total or cannot be checked. Both modes record an honest funding status for shared contributions. Turning off account tracking uses only the current character's carried gold. No gold is moved or reserved by the game; transfer gold from an alt before paying a hunter.

**WoW Forever realmsets:** private, guild, community and shared bounties, claims, portraits and suggestions are separated by the active PvE, PvP, RP, RP-PvP or Hardcore ruleset. Switching back restores that realmset's list. Internal realm suffixes stay in saved identities and network messages, but character names throughout the UI omit them. Guild sync also checks the realmset and requires updated clients. Older Forever lists without a recorded realmset remain in their original saved fields; they are hidden because their original ruleset cannot be determined. Recreate those bounties in their intended realmset.

**Claims & payments** on the bounty board (`/hg bounty claims`) keeps unsettled claims and recent settled ones (paid and rejected claims are trimmed beyond 200 entries; no new reports are accepted past 2,000), including the original offer, claimant and review status. Detected kills create reports automatically when an eligible reward exists. Use **View report** to follow the review, or **Report kill** / **Report a missed kill** for a kill the addon missed. Original posters approve or reject claims; contributors follow that decision and mark their own obligation **Paid** after paying manually. Unbound guild and community rewards also allow a moderator of the originating organization to review claims. Pending reports keep exported private rewards live until approval. The evidence distinguishes your locally confirmed killing blows from reports received from other players. A reported payment is a manual record. Removing or expiring the bounty keeps its claim history. No gold is transferred by the addon.

### Bounty suggestions

The visible **Suggestions** tab on the bounty board finds recurring open-world attackers. It is optional target discovery; tracking and claiming existing rewards do not require it. You can also open it with `/hg bounty suggestions`.

Suggestions and sharing your deaths both start **off**, with separate switches. In **Highground**, open `/hg config` → **Bounties → Settings** and find **Bounty suggestions**. Bounties must also be enabled. Suggestions work independently of the nearby panel and lifetime history.

The default personal trigger is **3 deaths to the same enemy in 30 minutes**. The guild trigger is **5 deaths across at least 3 guild characters in 30 minutes**, including your own deaths. Change both thresholds, the distinct victim count, the rolling time window (5–60 minutes) and the notification cooldown in settings. Exact names and realms remain separate; alts count as separate characters.

Each row groups one attacker and offers **View reports** with victim counts, times, locations and attribution sources. **Review bounty** opens a draft with the target, reason and audience filled in; reward starts blank. Personal incidents default to **Only me**, guild incidents to **Guild**. Saving or submitting is an explicit action. Existing known bounties open their listing instead, and guild submissions keep the usual officer approval rules.

Affected players and officers get a movable notification after combat, at most once per enemy per hour by default. Other members can review guild incidents quietly on the board. The notification closes after 20 seconds; reports remain on the board until they expire. **Dismiss 24h** suppresses that enemy for a day. **Ignore enemy** persists until you enable **Show ignored** on Suggestions and choose **Allow again**. The bounty text alert category also controls these notifications.

Guild reports use hidden addon messages. Each participating client sends only its own attributed death when sharing is enabled; it never reports a death on another character's behalf. You receive reports while online from guildmates with an updated compatible addon. Reports are player-supplied evidence, not server-verified kill proof. Duplicate and expired reports are discarded, and a guild change removes the old guild's reports. Recent records and per-character dismissals are stored in HighgroundBountyData and survive reloads.

Only attributable open-world deaths count. Cata uses fatal combat damage; Retail and WoW Forever use the latest readable death recap hit. NPCs, environmental deaths, pets without an identified player source, ambiguous names, duels and instanced deaths are skipped. Unavailable game data can leave a death uncounted. Lifetime enemy history uses the same conservative death attribution.

### Shared bounties

Wanted posters update their progress as kills are recorded and approved. Private bounties count recorded kills; guild and shared bounties separate approved kills from reports pending review, and rewards stop at the requested total. Where the combat log is readable, reports retain the fatal spell name and ID, melee or pet attack, or environmental cause. The cause appears in proof and claim details and travels with guild/shared reports to updated clients. Environmental deaths without hunter credit do not automatically claim a reward. Restricted clients, including Forever, display **Cause unavailable** when it cannot be observed. The latest 200 local kill observations are retained across reloads and separated by Forever realmset.

Open the shared bounty network with `/hg bounty shared`. Receiving and publishing both start off; choose them separately on the board or in settings. These choices control shared bounties independently of private alerts and guild submissions. To receive other players' rewards, use **Browse shared bounties** on the Shared board. Receiving and publishing remain independent choices.

To create an original reward, click **Create bounty**, choose **Shared**, and enter the exact target, a positive whole gold reward and **Expires after** duration (`30m`, `2h`, `7d`). Click **Enable sharing & publish**, or **Publish shared bounty** if already enabled. Durations range from five minutes to thirty days, with a configurable 24-hour default. **Notify me from** under **Settings → Shared alert settings** only changes notifications. Shared expiry offers horizontal **2h**, **24h**, **3d**, **7d**, and **Custom** choices. Custom accepts 5 minutes to 30 days. Gold balances display gold, silver and copper in their own colors in the form, account breakdown and bag tooltip.

To contribute to an existing reward, choose **Add gold** on its wanted poster. Review the original poster's name, completion terms and kill goal, then choose **Accept terms & add gold**. Their acceptance of a hunter's claim creates your payment obligation to the same hunter; contributors cannot approve or reject completion separately. Gold remains a manual payment through the game. Added gold keeps the original expiry and criteria. Contributions made after earlier approved kills fund only the remaining kills. A player can have one live commitment per enemy across audiences; subsequent additions increase that commitment.

**Mega bounty pot:** live personal, guild, community and global rewards for an exact enemy combine on the board and wanted posters, with each original reward's breakdown retained. Expired, withdrawn and completed rewards do not count toward the live pot, and guild/global copies of one reward count once. Each player has one live commitment per enemy: **Edit reward** changes your own amount, and **Add gold** on another player's reward binds you to that original's terms, expiry and reviewer. Independent original offers keep their own reviewers; an accepted claim makes every attached contribution payable to the same hunter. The contribution screen previews the new pot, quick amounts and your maximum commitment. Personal and guild originals use the configured bounty lifetime.

Contributions on the same exact character and realm stack. Alice's 500g for two hours plus Bob's 1,000g for one day initially shows 1,500g; Alice's contribution drops out when its own clock ends. **View bounty** shows each poster, amount, funding report and expiry together. A known target GUID also guards automatically observed kill claims against a different character with the same displayed name.

**Guild and Shared duplicates:** publishing your existing guild reward links both audiences to the same contribution ID. A 500g reward shown in Guild and Shared still owes 500g once; another poster adding 300g makes the shared total 800g. Sharing an existing public contribution with your guild also reuses its amount and ID. Guild approval still controls its guild listing. Guild members can receive the linked contribution and submit its claim without joining realm-wide discovery. Claims, funding, expiry and withdrawal use the same contribution; closing it ends hunts in both views. Removing only the guild listing leaves its shared contribution active. Resolve outstanding legacy guild claims before converting a reward. Linked guild listings require updated Highground clients; older clients do not understand their versioned guild message.

Each account has a persistent generated contributor ID and one active contribution per target. Increasing a pledge updates its existing ID and revision, so retries and synchronization never add the reward twice. Only the posting character can change or withdraw it. An independent original pledge can increase its amount or extend its expiry, up to thirty days after its original creation; completion notes and kill criteria stay fixed after publication. Added contributions keep the original expiry. Pending claims must be reviewed before edits or withdrawal.

**Funding** checks the account's recorded character gold for the current server or Forever realmset at publication, on login and whenever money changes. Account tracking is on by default; requiring sufficient gold is off by default. Other shared commitments, pending and approved unpaid claims, and recorded guild obligations across those characters reduce the available amount; the same offer and claim are counted once. Existing claims take budget priority, then older offers. A shortfall warns the poster and updates shared reports even when creation is permitted. Only the posting character advertises updates for its contributions. Other players receive only status and check time, never the wallet balance:

- **Funds reported available**: the poster's latest local check covers the commitment.
- **Insufficient funds**: the latest check found a shortfall.
- **Funds status unknown**: a check is unavailable or the report is older than five minutes.

The Shared board distinguishes all pledged gold from the total with recent available-funds reports. **Notify me from (gold)** applies to the latter, summed across contributors. Matching rewards appear first; smaller and unconfirmed rewards stay below them with muted text and an explanation. They remain searchable and their actions stay available. Notifications fire when the total crosses the threshold, with a two-minute cooldown; ordinary sightings also follow bounty alert and mute settings. These reports cannot guarantee payment: the game does not reserve gold, and another player's addon cannot independently verify the poster's report.

**Claims** award the listed gold per approved kill, up to the original's requested count. Different hunters can earn different kill slots. The bounty terms require the hunter's killing blow; participation alone does not automatically qualify. Cataclysm records the local player's killing-blow event when available. Retail and WoW Forever use named honor-credit reports when available, with manual reporting as a fallback. Honor credit can include assists. The original poster reviews the report and any externally posted proof. Their accepted decision makes attached contributions payable without a second approval from each contributor. A kill must occur before contribution expiry or withdrawal, and a new report must reach the original poster within fifteen minutes. Reports retry while connected; accepted decisions are retained and replayed when contributors reconnect.

Earlier kills remain eligible during that claim window, including after withdrawal. The original amount and terms are kept with the claim. Funding bookkeeping holds an expired or withdrawn pledge for the fifteen-minute claim window and keeps pending or approved debts afterward. Approving the final requested kill closes the contribution to new hunts and rejects remaining pending claims. Paying an earlier kill keeps the unfilled slots active. Approved debts cannot be cancelled by expiry, withdrawal or rejection. An unpaid contribution waits 90 days after it expires for a delayed decision from an offline original owner, then leaves the board so it cannot hold a slot forever. Pay through the game, then use **Mark paid**; the addon records the payment without transferring gold. Claimants request current status after reconnecting, including after a missed approval. The shared ledger keeps unsettled claims and up to 1,000 settled records.

**Reach and persistence:** sharing connects opted-in clients within the realm/faction chat boundary. Retail and WoW Forever use a dedicated addon channel. Forever separates bounty and user discovery by PvE, PvP, RP, RP-PvP and Hardcore rulesets; RP-PvP uses `HighgroundBountiesRPPvP` and `HighgroundUsersRPPvP`. Old memberships are left automatically, and joining waits if the ruleset is unavailable. Participants need updated addons to meet on the new channels; older versions use the former shared channels. Cataclysm discovers participants through a short message in `HighgroundBounties` and sends data by addon whisper. Messages are queued, bounded and retried; channel restrictions or combat messaging lockdown can delay delivery, and Refresh reports connection status. Online posters answer synchronization requests for their own contributions. Cached offline offers show unknown funds when reports age out; there is no central server guaranteeing delivery to offline players.

The included **HighgroundBountyData** dependency owns the account-wide ID, shared contributions and claim history. Keep it enabled. The ID handles ordinary alt reuse and duplicate messages; resetting that saved data or using another installation can create a new identity. It is not a verified Blizzard account identifier.

Command examples: `/hg bounty shared receive on`, `/hg bounty shared publish on`, `/hg bounty shared threshold 1000`, `/hg bounty shared add 500 2h target`, `/hg bounty shared claim NAME`, `/hg bounty shared withdraw NAME`, `/hg bounty shared sync`.

## Macros

**Macros** (`/hg macros`) holds ready-made PvP and PvE macros for your class and game, and a builder. Generated preset spell names resolve through spell IDs into your client's language when the spell is available; player-written scripts and imported bodies are kept as entered.

A macro puts one or more written game commands on an action-bar button. Saving one does not use the ability; you must drag its icon to an action bar and press it yourself.

- **Players (PvP)** is for fighting other players; **World (PvE)** is for quests, dungeons and raids. **Spell ranks** appears on clients with ranked spells.
- **For my class** starts with presets whose spells you know. **Utility** has macros for every class; **All classes** lets you browse others. Search filters the current tab and clears when you switch tabs.
- **My macros** contains your saved macros. **Create** opens the builder.

### Preset cards

1. Read the macro's one-line purpose and choose a target from the full-name menu (hover it to learn what the target means): for example **Focus**, **Mouseover**, **Target**, **Arena 1** or **Party 1**. All supported targets are in that menu. A focus is a second target: select a player and type `/focus` first.
2. Click **Use macro** to save it for this character. **Spell not learned** means a required spell is absent from your spellbook. Saving is disabled during combat.
3. Click **Open WoW macros**, then drag the saved icon onto an action bar. The message after saving repeats this next step.

A saved preset shows **Edit macro** and opens your existing text, preserving manual changes. **Saved with other text** means a macro with this name holds different text, for example the PvP version of a PvE preset. **More** offers **Customize macro**, **Save for all characters** and, when a character copy exists, an explicit **Replace saved macro with this preset**. When General storage already holds other text under that name, **Save for all characters** asks first: **Replace saved macro** or **Keep saved macro**. All-character copies use WoW's General macro storage; character copies use Character storage. Changing the target menu previews a version without rewriting a saved macro. Hover a card for its explanation. **Show macro code** at the bottom of the preset browser adds highlighted source to every preset card and remembers your choice. The preview follows the selected target; longer macros scroll inside the card. **Hide macro code** returns to the compact view. The preview shows the preset; **Edit macro** opens any saved manual changes.

### Create and edit

The setup and editor scroll independently. Inside the Highground window, the builder reclaims the duplicate title area to leave more room for your macro.

1. **Choose an action**: **Cast spell**, **Use item**, **Sequence** (`/castsequence`) or **Random** (`/castrandom`), then the spell, item or slot. Separate several spells with commas. In games with spell ranks, **Rank** pins a rank.
2. **Choose a target**: a menu names each target in full, including party members and the arena enemies available in your game. **Normal spell targeting** restores ordinary targeting. **A named group member** reveals a player-name field. The checkbox directly below the selected unit changes the recipient to that unit's target: **Use your focus's target** produces `@focustarget`, for example. It also works with mouseover, party and arena units, your current target, or a named group member; your own target stays selected. The recipient summary updates with the choice. Ground-at-cursor and self casts do not offer this option.
3. **Review & save**: edit or paste macro text directly, read **What this will do**, then choose **This character** (Character slots) or **All characters** (General slots) and **Save macro**. An existing name changes the action to **Update macro** and identifies the destination before saving. The note beside the button keeps the result: a confirmation, the reason a save was refused (such as a full macro tab), or that the destination already holds this exact text; Save stays off until you change something. **Open WoW macros** stays at the bottom, so you can drag the saved macro to an action bar.

**Target conditions**, **Stance or form**, **Casting options**, **Alternative action**, **Use items first**, **Announce** and **Utility action** unfold when needed. Their headers count active options even while folded.

- **Target conditions**: living-enemy/ally checks and fallbacks. A fallback runs when the earlier macro conditions fail, including a missing recipient; range, line of sight and cooldowns do not trigger it. A self fallback is unavailable when the normal-target fallback already covers every remaining case.
- **Stance or form** (classes with stances or forms): in another stance, a press switches first (`[nostance:3] Berserker Stance`); press again to cast.
- **Casting options**: **Stop my current cast first**, **Stop casting after spell**, **Never cut my channel** (`/stopmacro [channeling]`), an explicit auto-attack choice, toggles, cancelling buffs and, for Hunters, Warlocks and Death Knights, a pet order: attack the spell's enemy or follow you. **Stop casting after spell** starts off and adds `/stopcasting` at the end for **Cast spell**. It attempts to cancel a normal cast after trying the spell; it cannot check Nightfall or guarantee instant-only casting. Normal casts may continue, and existing casts or channels can be interrupted. The editor warns about this ordering. Test while stationary with and without the proc before relying on it.
- **Alternative action**: one button, two jobs. When Shift, Ctrl or Alt is held, when right-clicked or out of combat, the macro casts the spell on yourself, on your focus or at your cursor, casts another spell at the same targets, or, in games with ranks, a lower rank (`[mod:shift] Healing Wave(Rank 3); Healing Wave`). A rank beside the same spell without one is not flagged.
- **Use items first**: both trinket slots, comma-separated names or slot numbers, and gear to equip first, such as a shield for Shield Bash (name a one-handed weapon before the shield).
- **Announce**: a party, raid, instance, say or yell line after the cast. `%t` names your target and `%f` your focus; the default names the spell's recipient when it can. It is sent with every press, even when the spell fails. During boss encounters only group chat works.
- Choosing a sequence reveals its reset settings.

Lines you type by hand survive recipe changes. **Manual edits** identifies text that differs from the recipe; the explanation always describes the text being saved, and warns that the form may not reflect those edits. An empty macro is a draft; errors appear once there is text to check. Validation and explanations have their own scroll area, and the save controls stay visible. Saving is disabled for errors or during combat, with a visible reason. Loading a different macro clears the old recipe and selects that macro's save destination.

### My macros

Your existing macros show their storage destination and flag problems as **Needs editing**. **Edit macro** opens the saved text in **Create**. **More** offers copying to the other destination, which asks before replacing other text under the same name there, and **Export macro**. **Export all** and **Import** appear on this tab. **Open WoW macros** opens Blizzard's window.

Imports open a per-macro review: existing names start **Skipped**; choose **Replace / add**, **Skip** or **Rename**, then **Import selected**. Names, bodies and available slots are checked before writing. **Undo changes** (`/hg macros history`) keeps the last 40 addon writes, including imports and rank updates. Undo restores the previous body or removes a macro just created by Highground; because a restore cannot itself be undone, **Undo change** asks for a second click on the same entry. Restore the newest change first; later manual edits are protected. The history shows this character’s backups for the current faction and realm/ruleset, plus shared General macro backups. Character macros must be restored in their original context, outside combat.

### Spell ranks (WoW Forever)

A spell name without a rank, like `/cast Frostbolt`, casts your highest rank. Every preset is written that way. Action buttons and macros that name a rank, like `Frostbolt(Rank 3)`, stay on it.

- **Highest spell rank** (Macros → Settings, on by default): when you learn a new rank, action buttons on the rank it replaces move up to it. Buttons on lower ranks, kept on purpose to save mana, stay. **Also in macros** does the same for `Name(Rank N)` in your macros. Changes wait until you leave combat.
- **Spell ranks** (next to Players and World) lists a preset for every spell in your spellbook that has ranks. Each casts the spell by name, so it always uses your highest rank, also after you learn a new one. Heals and buffs go to the ally under your mouse, then your target, then you; other spells to your target, with focus and mouseover variants. `/hg macros presets` opens it.
- `/hg macros ranks` moves every button and macro on a lower rank up to your highest rank.
- Create has a **Rank** field: empty casts your highest rank; a number pins that rank. The checker warns when a macro pins a rank lower than one you know.

### Checks and autocomplete

Every macro is explained line by line in plain English. Problems are listed by line as you type, such as an unknown condition (`[@fokus]`), a missing `]`, more than 255 characters, a spell not in your spellbook, or a command your game lacks (`/ping` on Cataclysm). Macros with errors cannot be copied.

Autocomplete covers spells, items and slots, buffs to remove, commands, conditions and units, values such as `mod:shift`, raid markers (`/tm`), sequence resets (`reset=`) and `#showtooltip`. **Tab** accepts a suggestion; without an open list it moves between visible fields (Shift-Tab goes backwards). Completion appears beside the caret, above or below the current line, within the editor, so it does not cover the explanation. **Escape** dismisses completion first and leaves the editor on a second press. In the one-line fields, **Enter** also accepts and the arrow keys move through the list; in the macro box only **Tab** accepts, so **Enter** and the arrow keys keep their editing meaning.

## Teammate pressure shields (Cata)

Shield badges mark up to three teammates (`/hg pressure count N`, 1–3) with the most observed enemies targeting them. The number counts distinct enemies targeting that teammate, not damage. The shield turns from amber to red at three. Badges are private to you. Turn on friendly nameplates (usually Shift+V) to see them; your own badge sits left of the player frame when your nameplate is hidden.
