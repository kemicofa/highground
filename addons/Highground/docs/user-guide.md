## The Highground window

`/hg`, the minimap button, the **Open or close Highground** key binding or **Open Highground** in **Options → AddOns → Highground** open the same window. **Back** and **Forward** in the header revisit feature pages, drafts and hub sections from this UI session; standalone feature windows offer the same controls. Closing and reopening keeps that history; reloading the UI starts a new history at your last saved section. Its sidebar has:

- **Home**: which panels are enabled, **Back to game**, **Preview and move panels**, and shortcuts to addons and alerts.
- **Feature guide**: pick an addon to learn through actual controls, short explanations, highlights and a moving pointer (disabled by Reduced motion). Each subaddon card has an **Enable addon** checkbox with the same per-character setting as **Addons** and WoW's AddOn List. Changes preserve panel preferences and records; **Reload to apply** starts or stops the selected addons. Reverting a switch before reload cancels its pending change. Nearby and Arena also have a separate **Show panel** switch. **Guides**, **Replay**, **Back** and **Next** share one row; each guide saves its place. **Enable in game** inside a walkthrough saves that feature's preference immediately. Nearby includes **History → select an enemy → Add bounty**. Bounties offers **Post a bounty** for creation, claim review and payment records, or **Hunt a bounty** for discovery, tracking, kill reports and your own claims. Each path follows one target; gold **Next** demonstrates the highlighted sample action, and **Back** revisits your previous step. Imports and gold settings remain optional. Sample records stay separate; real settings and macro saves are labelled. Open `/hg guide`, `/hg guide macros` or `/hg guide bounties`; `/hg checklist` remains an alias.
- **Addons**: enable or disable Battlegrounds, Arena, Nearby, Bounties, Reputation and Macros for this character, then reload. Settings and records are preserved. If separate standalone downloads are also installed, a notice lists exactly which extras to remove and which bundled folders to keep. Home links to this notice too. **How addons work** explains the installation, real switches and reload status. Both required data addons must stay enabled.
- **Battlegrounds**, **Arena**, **Nearby**, **Bounties**, **Reputation** and **Macros**: equal sidebar sections with an overview and enable switch. Expand a section for its settings and tools.
- **Preferences**: Panels and features, Appearance and Alerts. Panel switches tune enabled modules; Addons controls whole modules.
- **Saved setups**: Playstyle presets and Profiles. Module enable states are separate from profiles.
- **Help**: **Addon users** and **Help and previews**. **Addon users** (`/hg users`) combines your character, the latest complete discovery-channel member list and addon announcements received in the last 15 minutes. Rows overlap; the total counts each character once. You count immediately while detection is enabled. Opening the page or pressing **Refresh members** requests a member list when available; requests are throttled and defer while WoW's channel UI is in use or messaging is locked. Versions arrive separately, and members without an announcement have their own count. A channel member does not necessarily have a responding addon. Incomplete or unavailable lists keep the previous complete list for up to six minutes, then fall back to announcements. Detections reset on reload; these counts are a sample, not a server census. Detection and realm discovery have separate switches. A reported newer release can trigger one notice per session and at most once per day; verify it in your addon manager. **Copy diagnostics** or `/hg users why` reports messaging restrictions, channel membership, roster refresh status and retries without sending messages or forcing a join. Try the isolated membership/departure/fallback sample with `/hg guide users`. See [discovery details](../../../docs/networking.md#addon-user-discovery).

The Blizzard settings panel has the same feature and tuning pages; **Panels and features** is called **General** there. The window works in combat, moves by its header, closes with Escape and reopens on the page you left.

The minimap's right-click menu offers lock/unlock, Highground, Addons, macros, the bounty board, Reputation and help. Drag the button to move it around the minimap. **Minimap button** in **Panels and features → Layout and sound** turns it off. Retail and WoW Forever also list Highground in the AddOns button beside the minimap.

To move panels, use **Preview and move panels** on Home. Examples appear outside a match; drag their headers and click **Done** to return to the game. Positions save automatically. You can also unlock panels from Home, the minimap menu or any panel's cogwheel. Move them outside combat, then lock them. Unlocking reveals headers even in Frameless mode.

Every separate HUD panel has a cogwheel for lock/unlock, arrangement, relevant quick actions and settings. A compact battleground column shares one on its first visible panel; if that panel disappears, another takes over. Locked cogs stay faintly visible and brighten when you hover the panel or column. They stay bright while unlocked or while their menu is open, and remain accessible in Frameless mode. Arena, nearby and standalone addons use the same controls. In combat, placement actions explain that movement must wait until combat ends.

## Features

Choose a group in **Panels and features**. Its main switches appear first; **More options** reveals extra markers and detailed controls. Switching groups folds those controls without changing their values. Disabled child switches explain which parent to enable. Every change saves immediately.

New installations include unrated battlegrounds, show five enemies, enable the target crosshair and Nearby enemies, and use **Essentials** alerts. Nearby starts with five rows and sighting/stealth sounds enabled. Burst markers, pressure shields, objective overview, sap calls and low-health resurrection reminders start off. The quest tracker stays visible. Upgrades enable unrated battlegrounds once for the account and saved setups, preserving other preferences. Later scope choices are kept. Shared-bounty receiving and publishing remain separate opt-ins.

**Include unrated battlegrounds** controls ordinary matches. Under **More options**, **10v10**, **15v15** and **Epic battlegrounds** filter team sizes; rated battlegrounds and Blitz use the 10v10 switch, Ashran the epic switch. **Sounds** mutes every Highground sound. Unsupported controls are hidden for your client.

### Set up for your role

**Saved setups → Playstyle presets** (`/hg setup`) offers **Minimal**, **Damage**, **Healer**, **Flag carrier** and **Caller**. **Use [playstyle] setup** saves a backup named **Before role preset**, changes battleground tools and alerts, then opens panel placement. Other choices, such as nearby enemies, arena panels and observation sharing, stay as you set them. **Move current panels** opens placement with your current settings.

Placement shows example data and draggable headers. Choose another panel set to arrange arena or nearby panels too. **Done** saves positions, locks panels and returns to the game. Closing placement, entering combat or joining a match stops previews and restores the earlier lock state. Named profiles are optional; ordinary settings always save automatically.

### Alert controls

`/hg alerts` offers **Essentials** (the new-install default), **Everything**, **Critical only**, **Objectives** and **Text only**. Essentials keeps objective warnings, attack openings, nearby sightings, stealth and bounty alerts while silencing target locks. Text only silences sounds.

**Customize categories** reveals separate text and sound switches. The selected alert level still applies to those categories. Changes save automatically. Sounds arriving together use the highest-priority cue; the general **Sounds** switch mutes them all.

**Enemy stealth** uses altered versions of the game's stealth sound: lower-pitched when applied, higher-pitched when removed. This works for nearby enemies, active battlegrounds, arenas and the current duel opponent. Nearby also follows its own sound switch. Cata reads applications and removals from the combat log; Retail and Forever use readable enemy casts and reappearance; Nearby also detects readable hiding auras. Restart WoW after installing the new sound files.

## Main panel

Enemy rows keep their positions by default while priority ranks and recommendations update. The initial roster is alphabetical; new arrivals append without moving existing enemies. Death, resurrection, range and priority changes do not reorder the list. Enable **Battlegrounds → Target behavior → Reorder enemy rows by priority (out of combat)** to make rows follow priority instead. Secure click bindings always stay fixed during combat.

The **suggested-target card** above the list is off by default. Enable **Appearance → Panels → Suggested target card** to show the recommended enemy's name, class, health and teammate pressure. Hiding the card keeps priority calculations, row highlights and recommendations active.

New installations show five enemy rows; change **Appearance → Enemies shown** or use the cogwheel to show five, ten or all enemies. A header such as **5/15** means five of fifteen are displayed. With stable ordering, a higher-priority enemy can be outside that limit; choose **All** to see the whole roster. Hover a row for the full spec and how it was found, location, cast, and cooldowns used with time remaining. Numerical scores are available in **Target behavior → Refresh comparison**.

### Enemy row legend

Some elements depend on your client, current observations and Appearance switches. Compact layout hides the priority tag and teammate count.

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Class-coloured background and narrow strip | The enemy's class. Highground and Class Colors keep the whole row tinted independently of health; Class Colors uses stronger tinting. |
| Number at the left | **Priority rank**, with 1 highest; this is not a score or a fixed row number. Ranks update while row positions stay fixed. By default they use base priority; the live-score option includes current combat signals. A brief green/red flash means the rank improved/worsened. |
| Name | Enemy player, with the local realm omitted. Other realms remain visible except on Forever, which hides realm suffixes. A faded name means no current unit observation. |
| Icon | Specialization when known; otherwise class. It becomes grey when dead. |
| Skull, cross or other marker over the icon | The team's raid target marker. |
| Spec text, such as `FROST`, `DISC` or `ARMS` | Short specialization name. `?` means unknown; `~` before the spec means estimated on Cata. Green/red indicates a favorable/unfavorable modeled matchup for your spec; muted text is neutral. |
| `CAP 3.2` / `CAP ~3.2` | Capturing a base, with seconds remaining; `~` marks an estimated timer. Replaces the spec text. |
| `FLAG`, `FLAG x3`, `ORB 30%` | Carrier information replaces the spec: flag carrier, flag debuff stacks, or extra damage taken from carrying an orb. |
| Health fill or thin line | Health when observable; a faded full bar means unknown health, not confirmed full health. |
| `TGT`, `FOC`, `T/F` | Your target, focus, or both. An ivory target edge and blue focus edge remain separate from class identity and the gold recommendation. Labels sit below the rank in expanded rows; compact rows keep the edges. |
| `72%` | Health percentage (Cata); red at or below the execute threshold, muted for a last-known reading. |
| Thin blue bottom bar | Mana for observed mana users (Cata); fades as the reading gets older. |
| Thin top bar | Cast/channel progress: amber if interruptible, grey if not. Also shows capture progress; an estimated capture bar is faded. |
| Plain range number, such as `30` | Nearest confirmed spell-range band in yards, not an exact distance. Bright within reach, muted beyond reach. `FAR` means visible without a confirmed range band; `--` means unknown. A green dot can display an in-range answer when the game hides its value. |
| `NOW` | The current reachable attack recommendation, also marked by an accent outline and rank tile. |
| `TOP` | Highest-ranked living enemy when someone else is the current attack recommendation. Older settings text called this `PRIO`. |
| `DEAD` | Enemy is dead; the row is dimmed and stays in place. |
| PvP trinket icon with a number | Cata trinket tracking: bright means ready or not observed used; grey plus a number means estimated seconds until ready. |
| `x3` beside the spec | Three teammates currently targeting this enemy. Highlighted from three upward; different from `FLAG x3` stacks. |
| Eye icon | This enemy is targeting you. |
| Thin pale outline and arrow outside the row | Your currently selected target. This can differ from the recommended target. |
| Red edge/glow | An attack opening after defensive protection ends. A separate red outline indicates health under the execute threshold; reduced motion makes animated cues steady. |
| Gold outline and coin | An active bounty on this enemy, when bounty markers are enabled. |
| Dimmed row | Dead enemies are strongly dimmed; defensive protection, immunity or preserved crowd control also dims content. An enemy lost while its combat click is frozen is faded further. |

The **status chip** shows one state at a time, in this precedence order:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **IMMUNE** | Known full immunity; never recommended |
| **DEF** | Defensive active |
| **CC** | Breakable crowd control when specifically identified, or a general crowd-control observation |
| **STUN/SIL** | Stunned or silenced (Cata) |
| **FLAG / ORB** | Flag or orb carrier |
| **SPENT** | Defensive protection recently ended; the attack opportunity is fading |
| **PURGE** | Offensive cooldown your class can remove (Cata) |
| **BURST** | Offensive cooldown active |

The specific breakable-CC observation takes precedence over `STUN/SIL`; a generic CC observation follows it. Retail and WoW Forever restrict enemy health and power readings, so readable health percentages, mana and combat-log trinket countdowns are Cata features. See [estimated specs](observations.md#estimated-specs-cata) for how Cata estimates specialization.

Left-click an enemy row to select it; right-click sets your focus, a second target kept separately from your current target. The card's name is its click target; shift-click calls it in chat. Outside combat, the card and the target key follow the recommendation. In combat, click bindings cannot change: if the recommendation changes, the card shows **CLICK TARGET · FIXED IN COMBAT** with **RECOMMENDED: name** below it, so use that enemy's row or nameplate. With no target bound before combat, the card shows **Target click unavailable** until combat ends.

An attack opening occurs when an enemy's observed defensive protection ends: the card shows **ATTACK · PROTECTION ENDED** and the alert can play a sound.

### Markers over the world

- **Target marker**: a transparent layered sight on the recommended enemy, or your own target when recommendations are off (**Sniper marker** on Features). The skull is centered inside the ring. Under **Features → Targets → More options**, switch **Skull in target marker** or **Animate target marker** independently; `/hg sniper skull on|off` and `/hg sniper animate on|off` do the same. The marker gently pulses every 2.4 s by default, then pulses every 1.8 s at 50% or less, 1.2 s at 30% or less, and 0.8 s at 15% or less. An active kill window also uses the fastest pulse. Unknown health keeps the gentle pulse unless there is a kill-window cue; an immune or dead target does not pulse. A high recommendation score alone does not imply a finishing opportunity. There is no rotation or zoom, and Reduced motion keeps the marker steady. **Marker placement**: **Appearance → Sniper marker → Marker style** offers **New icon (default)** or **Legacy crosshair** (`/hg sniper style layered|legacy`); both share the pulse and placement controls. A mini preview beside the style dropdown shows the selected icon and skull setting. **Colours**: the Ring / crosshair, Skull and Aiming mark / dot buttons open a colour picker with live previews; each has a Default button, and Cancel restores its previous colour. Custom colours persist during kill windows and save with profiles. Legacy supports separate crosshair and dot colours; its skull control is disabled. Commands: `/hg sniper color ring FF8800`, `/hg sniper color skull 33BB77`, `/hg sniper color aim default`. **Placement** offers Head area (default), Body (raised 22 px), Left of nameplate, Right of nameplate and Above nameplate. Adjust the edge gap, horizontal/vertical offsets and size there. Head area is an estimate below the nameplate, not a helmet attachment: camera angle, model size and stacked nameplates can move it away from the head. Left/right/above use the nameplate frame's edges; other nameplate addons may need offset adjustments. Gap applies to those three edge placements and stays constant when resizing. Positive X moves right and positive Y moves up. Changing placement restores its default height while keeping gap, X and size. Custom placement heights survive the upgrade; untouched old body defaults move to head area. Commands: `/hg sniper place head`, `/hg sniper place left`, `/hg sniper place right`, `/hg sniper padding 12`, `/hg sniper x 0`, `/hg sniper y 0`. Use `/hg sniper test` on a target with a visible nameplate and `/hg go` for a kill-window preview. Named ring colours (including `gold`) and lock-on sound remain configurable with `/hg sniper`. Fully restart WoW when first installing the textures; subsequent placement and animation code updates need only `/reload`.
- **Healer markers**: green crosses above friendly healers with a known group role, and red crosses above known enemy healers (spec from the scoreboard, a talent-tree cast on Cata, or a spec clue). Enable friendly nameplates with Shift+V to see your healers. Toggle these in setup or **Panels and features → Targets**. Unknown roles and unavailable nameplates show no marker.
- **Enemy burst markers** (Cata): while an enemy has a big offensive cooldown up, its icon sits beside their nameplate with **PURGE** if your class can remove it, otherwise **CC**. The **Burst markers** page sets each spell to Auto, Purge only, CC or Off.
- **Sap calls** (rogues, Warsong Gulch and Twin Peaks): when the enemy flag carrier or a known enemy healer dies, a raid warning says **SAP name at their graveyard**, with their resurrection countdown when known.

## Target recommendations

Turn off **Target recommendations** on Features (or `/hg priority off`) to pick your own targets. This removes the enemy panel and card, the target, focus and mark keys, the GO sound and the map's kill-target mark. The target marker stays and follows your own target.

**Target behavior** sets:

- **Switch margin** (default 8 points): how far ahead another enemy must be before the recommendation switches or rows reorder. A GO candidate must also be within it.
- **GO alert** (default 12 seconds after a defensive ends; 0 turns GO off) and the **defensive-spent** bonus, which fades over 60 seconds.
- **Reorder enemy rows by priority** (off by default): move rows to match rank outside combat. Turning it off keeps their current order.
- **Use live scores for priority ranks and sorting** (off by default): include current combat signals in the rank, rather than base priority alone. The attack recommendation always considers live signals, even with both sorting options off.
- On Cata, the **execute threshold** and **Preserve breakable crowd control** (on by default).

Known full immunities never win, whatever the points. Retail reads only clear aura categories and does not guess immunity or breakable CC.

The other targeting pages set point values: **Target priority**, **Objectives**, **Live signals**, **Cooldowns**, **Spec ratings** and **Advanced multipliers**. Hover a value to see its range; multipliers show the effective value (points × your multiplier × battleground modifier, for example a flag bonus of 30 × 1 × 1.5 = 45 in capture-the-flag maps). On **Cooldowns**, **Show spell overrides** lists per-spell values: leave a field blank to use the general value, type a number to fix it, press **x** to clear it.

Spec ratings (0–15) are starting points, not measured odds. Only Retail Frost Death Knight has hand-written matchups; other specs use generic armor, protection and mobility modifiers.

**Reset tuning** restores points, multipliers, spec ratings and target behavior. It keeps feature switches, appearance and cooldown tracking.

### Cooldown tracking (Cata)

The main panel tracks big defensives, offensive cooldowns, and long crowd control or silences (Blind, Smoke Bomb, Hammer of Justice, Strangulate). The arena panel separately tracks observed interrupts and diminishing returns. The **Cooldown tracking** page turns single spells on or off; search by spell, class or type (`offensive`, `defensive`) and use **Track shown** / **Untrack shown**. An untracked spell loses its main-panel display, score bonus and spent alert; full immunities still block recommendations. Choices survive reloads and **Reset tuning**, and are included in full profiles.

## Profiles

Your active settings save automatically, including after role setup. A **named profile** is a separate reusable copy: tuning, features, appearance, HUD positions, alerts and tracking preferences. On **Profiles**, enter a name and click **Save profile**. An existing name changes the button to **Update profile**. To restore a copy, choose it under **Load a saved setup** and click **Load profile**; selecting its name alone changes nothing.

A footer appears when a named or imported profile is active, or a profile switch is pending. **Modified** means your active settings differ from its saved copy; **Update profile** stores those edits in that copy. **Imported copy** identifies settings applied from an import, and ordinary settings without a named profile show no footer. Click the footer to open Profiles. A queued switch shows **After combat** while keeping the current profile name until the switch takes effect.

Profiles are account-wide, so another character can load one without overwriting its own saved profile. **Sharing and more options** explains what a profile contains and reveals import, export, chat sharing, deletion, automatic switching and tuning reset. Sharing uses the selected saved copy; update that profile first to include later edits. Macros, keybindings, bounty records, journal notes, match history, other profiles and automatic switching are excluded. Export shows a base64 string; Import can apply it or save it under a name. An invalid import changes nothing. Older tuning-only exports still work. Loading during combat waits until combat ends.

To share a setup in game, choose **Sharing and more options → Share in chat**, or type `/hg profile share [NAME]`. Press Enter to send the prepared link. Both players need Highground, must be online and must be able to whisper each other. Links last 30 minutes or until the sender reloads. Clicking opens a summary with **Save a copy** and **Use this setup**. Clipboard export is available for sharing outside the game.

On **Profiles**, expand **Sharing and more options**, then open **Automatic switching**. Choose a saved profile for **Battlegrounds**, **Arena** or **Open world**, then enable **Switch profiles automatically**. Choices save as you make them and apply to your current character and specialization. **Keep current settings** leaves an activity unassigned. Other characters keep their own choices; changing specialization or activity loads its assigned profile.

## Appearance

Open **Appearance** directly with any panel cogwheel's **Panel appearance** action. **Panel transparency** is on by default: HUD backgrounds use 65% of the chosen **Background opacity** while idle and brighten on hover or while the cogwheel menu is open. Text, icons and timers keep their normal visibility. Compact battleground columns brighten together. Turn this off for a steady background. The page also includes:

- **Theme**: **Highground** is the default: obsidian steel, luminous gold, sculpted borders and persistent class colours. **Class Colors** uses the same brand with stronger class tinting. Class identity remains visible at low, unknown or hidden health. **Ember**, **Parchment** and **Azeroth** remain available. Explicit saved theme choices, opacity and fonts are kept; an unset choice uses Highground. A switch applies at once, also in combat, without moving or resizing panels. Also `/hg theme NAME`.
- **Accessibility**: **Reduced motion** replaces pulses, rank flashes and target zoom with steady indicators. **Font** and **Text size** let you choose a face and enlarge the text. All themes share the same text sizes.
- **Frameless**: no panel background or border; title bars hide while panels are locked.
- **Suggested target card**: hide it; the list moves up and the keys keep working.
- **Scale** per panel, on top of the UI scale.
- **Sizes and spacing** for the panels and rows. Normal and compact layouts keep their own sizes.
- **Enemy rows**: show or hide each element (rank, spec icon and text, health bar and percent, mana, trinket, status, distance, priority tag, teammates).
- **Text and bars**: health style (**Fill the row** or **Thin line**), bar texture and health colours (class, faction, or by health left).
- **Borders**: size (0 for none) and colour.

**Fine-tune appearance** reveals sizes and spacing, individual enemy-row elements, text and bars, borders, and **Reset appearance**. These details stay folded until you want them. Sizes change out of combat; everything else at once. Reset appearance restores the layout and text defaults; your theme and Reduced motion preference are kept.

On **Panels and features**, choose **Layout and sound**: **Panel size** (50–200%, default 80%) sizes every panel. Scale changes made in combat apply when it ends. **Background opacity** (0–100%, default 100%) is on **Appearance**, alongside **Panel transparency**; both apply immediately, including in combat.

**Compact layout** makes main enemy rows and the suggested-target card one line and narrows the panels. Flag carriers keep a separate health strip. In battlegrounds, the carrier, resurrection and objective panels dock under the main panel; drag any of them to move the column. The Arena and duel panel stays on its own.

## Battleground panels

Resurrection and objective panels show countdowns over thin progress bars. Amber marks the last five seconds, `~` an estimate and `--` unknown. **YOU**, **OURS** and **THEIRS** say whose graveyard it is; hover for details. The **flag carrier panel** labels your carrier **ALLY** and the opposing carrier **ENEMY**. **No carrier seen** means no carrier has been observed; **Flag dropped** describes an observed drop. Use the panel to protect your carrier or stop theirs: left-click to target, right-click to focus. The optional low-health resurrection reminder reports timing without telling you to die. See [observations and timers](observations.md) for how positions and countdowns are worked out.

**Objective overview** (optional, under More options) adds a **PLAN** summary when a resource prediction is available, ownership of graveyard bases and enemy sightings from the last 30 seconds. Sightings are labeled with their age; an empty count means no recent observation. Friendly bases close to falling receive an urgent warning. Low-health resurrection reminders are suppressed while you carry a flag or orb.

On cart, Strand and epic maps, the objective panel also shows observed objective widgets, map landmarks and recent announcements, with a map-specific goal. It can display cart control, gate state, round text and reinforcements when the game exposes them. It does not invent delivery, gate or boss countdowns when the client supplies none.

### Localized announcements

Flag, orb and node messages recognize recorded formats for English, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese clients. Live node names identify the local objective. Unknown formats keep the existing API and map observations; custom server wording and locales without recorded formats still need in-client verification.

### Quick callouts

Enable **Panels and features → Battleground → Quick callout panel** for a separate movable panel. It starts off, including on upgrades. In named battleground areas, it shows your current location and **Help**, **1**, **2**, **3+** and **All clear** buttons. Each click immediately sends a message to battleground chat, such as `Need help — Farm (3+ enemies)`. **Help** omits the count; **All clear** sends `All clear — Farm` to say everything is okay there. A brief cooldown prevents double sends; existing chat drafts stay intact.

Two additional switches under **Quick callout panel** start off, including on upgrades:

- **Enemy destination callouts:** choose a destination under **Enemies going to**, then click **1**, **2** or **3+**. For example, while at Lumber Mill, choose Farm and click **2** to send `2 enemies going to Farm`. Selecting a destination does not send a message or change the local Help buttons. The selection resets between matches. Base destinations are available in Arathi Basin, Battle for Gilneas, Eye of the Storm and Deepwind Gorge; other locations come from the current map's landmarks, with localized names where available.
- **EFC route callouts:** click **Ramp**, **Graveyard** or **Tunnel** in Warsong Gulch, or **Ramp**, **Graveyard** or **Water** in Twin Peaks, to send messages such as `EFC going tunnel`. These are manual sightings; no detected carrier or open flag panel is required.

Drag the panel header while panels are unlocked; its position is saved per character. It stays separate in Compact layout. **Arrange panels** previews it outside a match with sending disabled. Local help follows your named area; enabled destination and EFC controls also work on roads and in midfield. The panel hides outside battlegrounds and works with the timers switched off. All its send buttons share the same cooldown. Disable **Quick callout panel** to hide it and its extra controls.

### Match history

`/hg history` keeps the last 100 observed PvP matches on the account. Search by map, result, character or specialization. Entries show duration, available personal scoreboard statistics and rating change, plus up to 200 observed objective events. **Full timeline** opens the events for copying. Leaving before an observed result is recorded as **Left early**; unavailable stats remain absent. The displayed win rate counts recorded wins and losses. **Undo delete** restores the most recently deleted entry while the window is available.

## Arena panel

The panel handles arena matches and duels on Retail and Cataclysm Classic, and duels on WoW Forever.

On **WoW Forever**, Arena mode is **dueling**. The **DUEL** panel opens when you send or receive a challenge, or the countdown starts. It shows one opponent, a short status, health, casts and crowd control when available. Left-click targets; right-click focuses. The opponent stays fixed when you change targets, and the panel clears when the duel finishes or is cancelled. `/hg arena test` previews one duelist on Forever; `/hg arena off` disables automatic display. Secure clicks are prepared before combat; a first signal during combat lockdown waits until combat permits the panel to open.

Rows use a large class/spec icon, name, solid health bar, PvP trinket, casts and **TGT**/**FOC** marks, without redundant class/spec text. Crowd control temporarily replaces the icon. Confirmed arena specs take priority; Forever can infer a likely spec from readable self-cast talent buffs or successful casts. Deeper talent clues win; equal conflicting clues keep the class icon. The tooltip explains estimates. In arena matches, fixed **A1–A3** labels on Retail and up to **A1–A5** on Cata use matching arena unit bindings for combat clicks. Unseen opponents keep their name and observed spec. Trinkets show client-reported timing only: **?** means unavailable, not ready. Automatic duel trinket tracking is not guaranteed on Forever. Retail sends restricted data directly to display widgets; Cata adds diminishing-return strength (**50%**, **25%**, **immune**), a conservative 20-second reset after overlapping control ends, and observed interrupt estimates. Talents and resets can shorten those cooldowns. Duels do not enter match history.

## Nearby enemies

Outside battlegrounds, arenas, dungeons and raids, **Nearby enemies** lists hostile players you have seen, newest first: a large class icon, name, level and sighting age. Hiding spells or an earlier location supply context; full location stays in hover details. Hover for guild and encounter history. The panel starts on in new installations; toggle it in **Panels and features** or with `/hg nearby on|off`. Existing saved choices are kept. Left-click targets, right-click focuses and returns to your previous target. Sources are nameplates, your target, focus and mouseover; on Cata the combat log adds enemies without a visible nameplate. The **Nearby enemies** page sets how many are listed and when rows fade and disappear, plus the new-enemy sound. Enemies first seen in combat show as **+N after combat** in the header until combat ends.

The **NEARBY: N** header counts distinct visible, living enemy players across nameplates, target, focus and mouseover. The list and count include visible opposing-faction players on flight paths even when they cannot be attacked. It updates on sightings, nameplate departures and once a second, including during combat, and includes enemies beyond the row limit. The counter stays visible at **0** while enabled in the open world. Remembered rows and combat-log-only sightings do not increase it; those rows keep their normal fade and forget times. Target, focus and mouseover sightings stay fresh while the enemy remains visible. Clearing recent sightings leaves the live counter running.

New enemies play a soft two-note chime, limited to one sighting sound per ten seconds. **Sound: on/off** at the bottom of the panel controls nearby sounds. It starts enabled on new installations and preserves existing preferences; global alert levels, category switches and per-enemy mutes still apply.

When a nearby enemy uses Stealth, Prowl, Vanish, Invisibility, Shadowmeld or Camouflage, chat says so, and again when it fades. Distinct lower- and higher-pitched stealth cues accompany entry and exit. On Cata the combat log confirms both; on Retail and WoW Forever readable casts or hiding auras on visible enemies reveal entry, and visible reappearance without a hiding aura indicates exit. Unreadable game data cannot confirm a transition.

History belongs to the current character, faction and realm or Forever ruleset. It remembers every enemy that character meets: how many times, how often they killed you and you them. Encounter totals and kills stay in the hover details and journal; an open profile updates when a kill or death is recorded. Routine sighting messages follow your alert level. These are remembered sightings, not proof that someone is still nearby. Cata counts your killing blows from the combat log. Retail and WoW Forever count honorable-kill messages that name an enemy in your sightings or saved history; a missing realm is matched only when the name identifies one enemy. Generic honor gains and ambiguous names add no kill. On those clients, a death counts when the game's death recap names the enemy. `/hg nearby forget` clears only that character’s current history. Older records without an identifiable owner and ruleset stay preserved in SavedVariables but are hidden. See [data scopes](data-scopes.md) for the boundaries used throughout the addon.

Click **History** at the bottom of the nearby panel, even when the live list is empty, or open `/hg journal` to search enemy encounters by name, class, guild, area or note. **Previous/Next** browses up to 1,000 saved enemies across sessions, most recently seen first. Add a personal note, mute that player's encounter alerts, start a bounty or delete an entry. Muting keeps the player's history and suppresses nearby, stealth and bounty sighting alerts for them. Search has a **Clear search** action; paging selects a visible entry. **Undo delete** restores the last removed record without replacing a newer encounter with that player.

The test board starts with three distinct targets per audience, varied rewards, shared contributions and a pending guild submission. Standalone Bounties uses `/hgb test`; Nearby uses `/hgn bounty test`. Sample actions leave real rewards, settings, claims and drafts unchanged; they send no messages and take no screenshots. Dragging a test poster saves its position for future live alerts. Start previews out of combat; entering combat closes the sample session.

It covers nearby alerts and markers, wanted cards and full portraits, creation and kill/expiry terms, account gold and the bag tooltip, guild approval, shared arrivals and filters, adding gold to a pot, sharing/importing, killing blows and screenshot states, claims/payments, and death-report suggestions. Examples stay open while you explore them. Try the sample forms and switches, **Start tracking / Ignore**, repeated kills, screenshot failure/retry, and approving, rejecting or marking sample claims paid. They use separate sample windows: your real drafts, gold, settings, bounties and claims are unchanged. No screenshot is taken and no message is sent to other players. Copyable sample posts contain a tour-only code that cannot import into your real board. The portrait uses your selected player or your own character, labelled as a sample. Visuals work even with live alerts disabled; sound follows your settings.

Jump directly with `/hg bounty guide nearby`, `board`, `create`, `gold`, `guild`, `shared` (or `world`), `pot`, `exchange`, `kill`, `claims`, or `suggestions`. **Return** or Escape in a sample dialog brings you back; **Close tour**, **Finish tour**, or `/hg bounty guide stop` closes every sample. Running the command again while a sample is open also closes the tour. When launched from the full feature guide, closing it returns to that guide. Standalone Bounties uses `/hgb guide`; Nearby uses `/hgn bounty guide`, with the same chapters.

Put a bounty on an enemy player, with or without gold. When the target shows up in the open world, a battleground or an arena, Highground plays a sound, shows a wanted poster and puts a gold coin beside their nameplate. Their Highground rows get a gold frame and their tooltip names the reward. Highground keeps the list; players pay the gold themselves.

The compact parchment popup shows the target, reward and sighting location without an internal scrollbar. **View bounty** keeps full notes, reward terms and contributions in a compact summary, with one target header for multiple contributions. A visible player supplies their actual character portrait, retained locally so **View bounty** can still show it after they disappear. Their observed race and body type are saved even when the client cannot provide a creature model ID, as can happen with player models on WoW Forever. These details and any available model ID are included in guild sync and shared-bounty messages. After a reload, or on a recipient who has never seen the target, the portrait tooltip’s **RACE PORTRAIT** label identifies generic race artwork and **MODEL LIKENESS** identifies a reconstruction from a saved model. Neither reproduces the player's full face customization, hair or gear; seeing the player replaces it with the actual portrait. With no usable saved likeness, the card uses a class icon, or a coin if the class is unknown. WoW does not expose the rendered face as a portable image, and a player GUID alone cannot recreate it. Targets observed before race data was saved need another sighting to acquire this fallback.

Posters close after ten seconds; hover to pause, drag to save a new position, dismiss with **×**, or choose **View bounty**. Simultaneous sightings queue up to three more posters; queued sightings older than thirty seconds are discarded. The existing re-alert delay, journal mutes, shared reward minimum and alert filters still apply. Portrait observations use the existing guild-sharing and public publishing/receiving preferences. A poster records a sighting, not proof the enemy remains nearby.

**Bounty details** uses the same parchment wanted cards, with the reward shown once per contribution. Shared bounties show each contributor's note, funding and expiry on their own card, plus a labelled **Total pledged** when there is more than one contribution. Cards stack at larger text sizes; scroll to reach every contribution. Portraits update when the matching character becomes available, and a captured likeness stays on that character's card while browsing. **First spotted** preserves the original area, zone, reporter and time through reloads, sync and exports. **Last seen by you** distinguishes your observation from another hunter's **Latest report**. Tracking automatically shares visible open-world sightings with the bounty's audience; guild reports follow the guild-sharing preference and private sightings stay local. Recent reports in your zone rise to the top of the board and show a **Reported in your zone** poster; distant trackers get a chat update. Same-zone priority lasts two minutes and does not imply a measured distance. Existing alert filters, ignores and mutes apply; reports are throttled and never automatically forwarded. Unseen named targets have no invented origin.

Open the **bounty board** with `/hg bounty`, its key binding, the **Guild bounties** settings page, or a player's right-click menu (Retail and WoW Forever). An empty personal board offers **Create first bounty**. Choose the **Shared board** tab to hunt for other players. **Help** brings the guide back without losing your search. Start with **Only me** and no reward if you simply want alerts for a player.

The three main views are **My bounties**, **Guild & communities** and **Shared board**. Select a guild or native WoW character community in the middle view; each has its own board, including communities with identical names. Each shows one parchment wanted card per exact target, with its portrait, reward and audience: **Only me**, **Guild**, the selected community, **Shared**, or a linked organization/shared audience. Click a card or **View bounty** to open the full wanted poster popup; closing it restores the same board and scroll position. My bounties shows your targets, tracked rewards and rewards you posted; the shared total includes other contributors. The guild/community view has an **Awaiting approval** filter for your submissions and, for moderators, the review queue. Search finds players, realms, posters and audiences. **Import bounty** stays visible on the board. The visible **Suggestions** tab offers optional targets from death reports; settings stay under **Settings**.

**Create bounty** has three steps: **Target → Reward → Review**. Choose the player and audience, then enter gold per kill (`1` or `1g`), shared expiry and an optional note. One kill and any finish are the defaults; **Customize hunt** exposes additional conditions. The review shows the target, audience, terms and full commitment. Nothing is posted before the final confirmation. **Back** keeps the draft; **Cancel** on the first step discards it. Shared rewards need at least 1g; private tracking can be free. Organization submissions require membership. **Use target** accepts another player, including friendly or unflagged players; an invalid target leaves the draft intact.

- **Only me → Save private bounty** saves a private target. Gold is optional.
- **Guild / community → Submit bounty** submits to the selected guild or community. Gold is optional. Guild officers and community owners, leaders and moderators approve submissions; ordinary members submit for review.
- **Shared → Enable sharing & publish** opts into publishing and creates the contribution in one explicit action. Once enabled, the action says **Publish shared bounty**. Invalid drafts do not change the opt-in, and publishing does not automatically enable receiving.
- Cards show **TRACKING** or **NOT TRACKING**. **Start tracking / Stop tracking** changes sighting alerts without pledging gold or deleting rewards. **View report**, **Review report** and **Record payment** appear when a report needs attention. Open **View bounty** for visible **Share bounty**, **Edit reward** or **Claim reward** actions. Your own rewards use **Edit reward**; other players can contribute with **Add gold**. **Manage bounty** lists audience changes, tracking and removal directly. There are no card or poster “More” menus.
- **View bounty** opens the selected target's wanted poster popup: reward, audience, every contribution in this view, funding, expiry and notes. There is no second list to select from. **Share bounty** for private and group rewards also offers **Share in chat**, which prepares a message for you to send. Recipients review it before importing a private copy; an existing private bounty is replaced only after confirmation. `/hg bounty share NAME` prepares the same link.
- **Settings → Shared alert settings** controls shared browsing, advertising and the notification minimum. **Claims & payments** opens kill reports and payment records. **Refresh** requests updates from online participants and appears when that board can receive updates.
- Guild and community cards show whether moderator approval is pending or complete. This status applies to that listing; a linked shared contribution can already be published. Moderators can reject or remove a listing; submitters can remove their own.
- Officers are the guild master and the ranks with the officer checkbox in the guild's rank permissions. If the game doesn't provide rank permissions, the ranks up to **Officers** on Guild bounties count instead (default 1, the rank below the guild master).
- Guild and community bounties expire after 14 days by default. Online members exchange updates. Native community membership and roles are checked again when sending or receiving updates; communities can span realms without enabling global sharing. Clients without native communities offer the guild board.
- Open-world sightings of guild bounty targets are shared with the guild. On Cata, your killing blow on one tells the guild.
- WoW Forever first-and-last names work as typed (`Iron Fist`).

**Kill goals and preferred finishes:** new bounties default to one kill and any finish. **Customize hunt** exposes the 1–20 kill goal and optional preferred finishes; editing custom terms expands them automatically. Gold is per approved kill, and the form shows the full commitment. Preferred finishes need evidence of the setup and death; notes and bonuses are reviewed separately. Published shared kill counts and preferences cannot be edited; withdraw and create a new contribution to change them.

**Mega bounties:** live personal, guild, community and global rewards for the same enemy combine into one pot, counting linked copies once. Each player has one live commitment per enemy. **Edit reward** changes your own amount. **Add gold** is available on other players’ rewards when you have no existing commitment; contributions follow the original poster’s terms and expiry. Their accepted claim makes every attached contribution payable to the same hunter; independent original rewards retain their own reviewers. The contribution screen previews the new pot as you choose an amount, including quick amounts and your maximum commitment. It identifies the reviewer and audience. **Select link to copy**, then Ctrl+C, copies an importable bounty link; the open share window refreshes when contributions change. Copy and repost the new link to update Discord or other posts. Private/guild exports keep their audience, and payment remains manual.

**Kill detection and screenshots:** readable combat-log killing blows are confirmed locally; named honor credit on restricted clients may include assists and stays labelled **HONOR CREDIT**. Detected credit automatically reports all eligible rewards for the exact target, including imported private rewards. **View report** opens that death’s evidence and reward status without creating another claim. If detection missed a kill, use **Claim reward**, or **Report a missed kill** for another death. Reward approval and payment remain separate. The screenshot checkmark sits beside its explicit status and appears only after the game confirms saving; failures offer **Retry screenshot**. Images stay in `WoW/Screenshots`. **Copy proof (optional)** prepares text for sharing; only global proof offers Discord destinations.

**Exporting and importing:** **View bounty → Share bounty**, or `/hg bounty export NAME`, produces a bounty link labelled `PRIVATE`, `GUILD`, `COMMUNITY` or `GLOBAL`. Paste the bounty link, complete shared post or older code into the board’s **Import bounty** button, or `/hg bounty import`, review its audience and terms, then confirm. Existing private entries require replacement confirmation. Guild and community exports import as private drafts and need separate submission and moderator approval before publication. Community exports copy the selected reward and grant no native membership or approval. Private, guild and community exports have no global Discord posting flow. Short in-game chat links also identify their source audience; older links still import as private copies.

**Global bounties on Discord:** publishing returns to the board. Open **View bounty → Share bounty** for optional sharing. One bounty link is selected by default: press Ctrl+C, send it, and have the recipient choose **Import bounty → Review import**. **Select link to copy** reselects that same link. **Discord channel** opens a separate dialog with the destination address. Private and group rewards offer **Share in chat**. Older codes and complete posts still work for import. The link and raw code contain the same bounty snapshot; the link is for addon import, not a browser. Discord posting is optional because the reward is already published in game. The [Highground Discord](https://discord.gg/xu3VsxRjca) has matching `#bounties-pve`, `#bounties-pvp`, `#bounties-rp` and `#bounties-rp-pvp` boards. Hunters can import while the creator is offline; this enables receiving, not publishing, and preserves the creator, expiry and terms. Imported funding and identity remain unverified until the posting character sends an authenticated update. Imports cannot overwrite authenticated records, extend an expiry, grant review authority or cross game/region/realmset/faction boundaries. Explicit imports remain eligible for sighting alerts at their stated amount; network offers use funded totals. Repost an updated link when the bounty changes.

Global killed posters link to the matching `#bounty-proofs-*` channel. Each channel has a pinned template for the original bounty post, contribution ID, target/hunter, region and ruleset, kill number, time, location, preferred finish and attached screenshot/video. The addon does not upload or automatically post to Discord.

**New bounty matches:** incoming funded shared rewards and approved guild/community rewards that meet your minimum show a **NEW BOUNTY · FILTER MATCH** poster. It explicitly says the target has not been spotted. **Start tracking** adds the target to My bounties without pledging gold. **Ignore bounty** silences arrival and sighting alerts for that target; use **Start tracking** on its card to resume. Closing the poster keeps the board entry. Choices survive reloads and remain separate between Forever realmsets. Notification preferences still apply.

**Account gold** tracking starts **on**. **Gold & settings** in the creation form, **Settings → Account gold & funding**, or `/hg bounty gold` shows each character's gold, the total, existing commitments and the amount available for a new reward. Opening it from an editor preserves the draft; **Return** takes you straight back to it. Hover the gold in your bags for the same breakdown. Log into each character once to record its balance; the current character updates when money changes, while alts show their last recorded balance. Characters share a gold pool only within the same server, or the same active realmset in WoW Forever. The ledger is local to your WoW account and installation, shared by Highground, Bounties and Nearby. It never sends character balances to other players. Account-bank and guild-bank gold are not included.

**Require enough gold before creating a bounty** starts **off**. The reward field warns when the available amount is too low, and you can still create the bounty. Turn the setting on in **Gold & settings** to block rewards that exceed the available total or cannot be checked. Both modes record an honest funding status for shared contributions. Turning off account tracking uses only the current character's carried gold. No gold is moved or reserved by the game; transfer gold from an alt before paying a hunter.

**WoW Forever realmsets:** private, guild, community and shared bounties, claims, portraits and suggestions are separated by the active PvE, PvP, RP, RP-PvP or Hardcore ruleset. Switching back restores that realmset's list. Internal realm suffixes stay in saved identities and network messages, but character names throughout the UI omit them. Guild sync also checks the realmset and requires updated clients. Older Forever lists without a recorded realmset remain in their original saved fields; they are hidden because their original ruleset cannot be determined. Recreate those bounties in their intended realmset.

The **Guild bounties** page sets the sound, wanted poster popup, telling your group (off by default), sharing sightings, the re-alert delay, the fallback officer rank, the expiry, and **Also alert for bounty targets you can't attack**. On Features, **Bounties** turns the feature off and **Bounty markers** hides the coin and row frames.

Bounty details use a compact target summary, with the portrait beside the name and reward. Multiple contributions share one target header. Names, notes and conditions wrap without truncation; long details can scroll within the overview, while standalone wanted popups have no scrollbar. The editor, settings and **Claims & payments** share the board window. **Back/Forward** preserve drafts and filters; **Back to board** discards the current draft. Saving returns to My bounties. **Start tracking** is directly available on other players’ cards; after detection, **View report** opens the existing report.

**Claims & payments** on the bounty board (`/hg bounty claims`) keeps unsettled claims plus recent settled history (up to 2,000 local reports), including the original offer, claimant and review status. Detected kills create reports automatically when an eligible reward exists. Use **View report** to follow the review, or **Report kill** / **Report a missed kill** for a kill the addon missed. Original posters approve or reject claims; contributors follow that decision and mark their own obligation **Paid** after paying manually. Unbound guild and community rewards also allow a moderator of the originating organization to review claims. Pending reports keep exported private rewards live until approval. The evidence distinguishes your locally confirmed killing blows from reports received from other players. A reported payment is a manual record. Removing or expiring the bounty keeps its claim history. No gold is transferred by the addon.

### Bounty suggestions

The visible **Suggestions** tab on the bounty board finds recurring open-world attackers. It is optional target discovery; tracking and claiming existing rewards do not require it. You can also open it with **Highground: `/hg bounty suggestions`**, **Highground Bounties: `/hgb suggestions`**, or **Highground Nearby: `/hgn bounty suggestions`**.

Suggestions and sharing your deaths both start **off**, with separate switches. In **Highground**, open `/hg config` → **Guild bounties** and find **Bounty suggestions**. In standalone **Bounties**, use `/hgb config` → **More settings**; in standalone **Nearby**, use `/hgn config` → **More settings**. Bounties must also be enabled. Suggestions work independently of the nearby panel and lifetime history.

The default personal trigger is **3 deaths to the same enemy in 30 minutes**. The guild trigger is **5 deaths across at least 3 guild characters in 30 minutes**, including your own deaths. Change both thresholds, the distinct victim count, the rolling time window (5–60 minutes) and the notification cooldown in settings. Exact names and realms remain separate; alts count as separate characters.

Each row groups one attacker and offers **Reports** with victim counts, times, locations and attribution sources. **Review bounty** opens a draft with the target, reason and audience filled in; reward starts blank. Personal incidents default to **Only me**, guild incidents to **Guild**. Saving or submitting is an explicit action. Existing known bounties open their listing instead, and guild submissions keep the usual officer approval rules.

Affected players and officers get a movable notification after combat, at most once per enemy per hour by default. Other members can review guild incidents quietly on the board. The notification closes after 20 seconds; reports remain on the board until they expire. **Dismiss 24h** suppresses that enemy for a day. **Ignore enemy** persists until you enable **Show ignored** on Suggestions and choose **Allow again**. The bounty text alert category also controls these notifications.

Guild reports use hidden addon messages. Each participating client sends only its own attributed death when sharing is enabled; it never reports a death on another character's behalf. You receive reports while online from guildmates with an updated compatible addon. Reports are player-supplied evidence, not server-verified kill proof. Duplicate and expired reports are discarded, and a guild change removes the old guild's reports. Recent records and per-character dismissals survive reloads and switching bounty clients through HighgroundBountyData.

Only attributable open-world deaths count. Cata uses fatal combat damage; Retail and WoW Forever use the latest readable death recap hit. NPCs, environmental deaths, pets without an identified player source, ambiguous names, duels and instanced deaths are skipped. Unavailable game data can leave a death uncounted. Lifetime enemy history uses the same conservative death attribution.

### Shared bounties

Shared expiry offers horizontal **2h**, **24h**, **3d**, **7d**, and **Custom** choices. Custom accepts 5 minutes to 30 days. Gold balances display gold, silver and copper in their own colors in the form, account breakdown and bag tooltip.

Wanted posters update their progress as kills are recorded and approved. Private bounties count recorded kills; guild and shared bounties separate approved kills from reports pending review, and rewards stop at the requested total. Where the combat log is readable, reports retain the fatal spell name and ID, melee or pet attack, or environmental cause. The cause appears in proof and claim details and travels with guild/shared reports to updated clients. Environmental deaths without hunter credit do not automatically claim a reward. Restricted clients, including Forever, display **Cause unavailable** when it cannot be observed. The latest 200 local kill observations are retained across reloads and separated by Forever realmset.

Highground, Highground Bounties and Highground Nearby share the same bounty network. Open `/hg bounty shared`, `/hgb shared` or `/hgn bounty shared`. Receiving and publishing both start off; choose them separately on the board or in settings. These choices control shared bounties independently of private alerts and guild submissions.

To create an original reward, click **Create bounty**, choose **Shared**, and enter the exact target, a positive whole gold reward and **Expires after** duration (`30m`, `2h`, `7d`). Click **Enable sharing & publish**, or **Publish shared bounty** if already enabled. Durations range from five minutes to thirty days, with a configurable 24-hour default. **Notify me from** under **Settings → Shared alert settings** only changes notifications.

To contribute to an existing reward, choose **Add gold** on its wanted poster. Review the original poster's name, completion terms and kill goal, then choose **Accept terms & add gold**. Their acceptance of a hunter's claim creates your payment obligation to the same hunter; contributors cannot approve or reject completion separately. Gold remains a manual payment through the game. Added gold keeps the original expiry and criteria. Contributions made after earlier approved kills fund only the remaining kills. A player can have one live commitment per enemy across audiences; subsequent additions increase that commitment.

**Mega bounty pot:** live personal, guild, community and global rewards for an exact enemy combine on the board and wanted posters, with each original reward's breakdown retained. Expired, withdrawn and completed rewards do not count toward the live pot. Guild/global copies of one reward count once. Independent original offers keep their own reviewers; adding gold binds the contributor to the selected original. Personal and guild originals use the configured bounty lifetime.

**Updated bounty links:** **Select link to copy**, then Ctrl+C, copies a `highground://bounty/` link to paste in **Import bounty**. The open export refreshes when the pot changes. Copy and repost the updated link or complete post to Discord; previously pasted links remain snapshots. Pot exports use `HGB3` and keep original reviewers, contributions and terms. Older `HGB2` exports remain importable. Imports can refresh earlier unverified snapshots, while stale links cannot roll back authenticated records. Private and guild exports retain their audience, and global exports omit private rewards. These are addon import links, not hosted web pages.

To receive other players' rewards, use **Browse shared bounties** on the Shared board. Receiving and publishing remain independent choices.

Contributions on the same exact character and realm stack. Alice's 500g for two hours plus Bob's 1,000g for one day initially shows 1,500g; Alice's contribution drops out when its own clock ends. **View bounty** shows each poster, amount, funding report and expiry together. A known target GUID also guards automatically observed kill claims against a different character with the same displayed name.

**Guild and Shared duplicates:** publishing your existing guild reward links both audiences to the same contribution ID. A 500g reward shown in Guild and Shared still owes 500g once; another poster adding 300g makes the shared total 800g. Sharing an existing public contribution with your guild also reuses its amount and ID. Guild approval still controls its guild listing. Guild members can receive the linked contribution and submit its claim without joining realm-wide discovery. Claims, funding, expiry and withdrawal use the same contribution; closing it ends hunts in both views. Removing only the guild listing leaves its shared contribution active. Resolve outstanding legacy guild claims before converting a reward. Linked guild listings require updated Highground clients; older clients do not understand their versioned guild message.

Each account has a persistent generated contributor ID and one active contribution per target. Increasing a pledge updates its existing ID and revision, so retries and synchronization never add the reward twice. Only the posting character can change or withdraw it. An independent original pledge can increase its amount or extend its expiry, up to thirty days after its original creation; completion notes and kill criteria stay fixed after publication. Added contributions keep the original expiry. Pending claims must be reviewed before edits or withdrawal.

**Funding** checks the account's recorded character gold for the current server or Forever realmset at publication, on login and whenever money changes. Account tracking is on by default; requiring sufficient gold is off by default. Other shared commitments, pending and approved unpaid claims, and recorded guild obligations across those characters reduce the available amount; the same offer and claim are counted once. Existing claims take budget priority, then older offers. A shortfall warns the poster and updates shared reports even when creation is permitted. Only the posting character advertises updates for its contributions. Other players receive only status and check time, never the wallet balance:

- **Funds reported available**: the poster's latest local check covers the commitment.
- **Insufficient funds**: the latest check found a shortfall.
- **Funds status unknown**: a check is unavailable or the report is older than five minutes.

The Shared board distinguishes all pledged gold from the total with recent available-funds reports. **Notify me from (gold)** applies to the latter, summed across contributors. Matching rewards appear first; smaller and unconfirmed rewards stay below them with muted text and an explanation. They remain searchable and their actions stay available. Notifications fire when the total crosses the threshold, with a two-minute cooldown; ordinary sightings also follow bounty alert and mute settings. These reports cannot guarantee payment: the game does not reserve gold, and another player's addon cannot independently verify the poster's report.

**Claims** award the listed gold per approved kill, up to the original's requested count. Different hunters can earn different kill slots. The bounty terms require the hunter's killing blow; participation alone does not automatically qualify. Cataclysm records the local player's killing-blow event when available. Retail and WoW Forever use named honor-credit reports when available, with manual reporting as a fallback. Honor credit can include assists. The original poster reviews the report and any externally posted proof. Their accepted decision makes attached contributions payable without a second approval from each contributor. A kill must occur before contribution expiry or withdrawal, and a new report must reach the original poster within fifteen minutes. Reports retry while connected; accepted decisions are retained and replayed when contributors reconnect.

Earlier kills remain eligible during that claim window, including after withdrawal. The original amount and terms are kept with the claim. Funding bookkeeping holds an expired or withdrawn pledge for the fifteen-minute claim window and keeps pending or approved debts afterward. Approving the final requested kill closes the contribution to new hunts and rejects remaining pending claims. Paying an earlier kill keeps the unfilled slots active. Approved debts cannot be cancelled by expiry, withdrawal or rejection. Pay through the game, then use **Mark paid**; the addon records the payment without transferring gold. Claimants request current status after reconnecting, including after a missed approval. The shared ledger keeps unsettled claims and up to 1,000 settled records.

**Reach and persistence:** sharing connects opted-in clients within the realm/faction chat boundary. Retail and WoW Forever use a dedicated addon channel. Forever separates bounty and user discovery by PvE, PvP, RP, RP-PvP and Hardcore rulesets; RP-PvP uses `HighgroundBountiesRPPvP` and `HighgroundUsersRPPvP`. Old memberships are left automatically, and joining waits if the ruleset is unavailable. Participants need updated addons to meet on the new channels; older versions use the former shared channels. Cataclysm discovers participants through a short message in `HighgroundBounties` and sends data by addon whisper. Messages are queued, bounded and retried; channel restrictions or combat messaging lockdown can delay delivery, and Refresh reports connection status. Online posters answer synchronization requests for their own contributions. Cached offline offers show unknown funds when reports age out; there is no central server guaranteeing delivery to offline players.

The included **HighgroundBountyData** dependency owns the account-wide ID, shared contributions and claim history. Keep it enabled, including when switching between Nearby and Bounties. The ID handles ordinary alt reuse and duplicate messages; resetting that saved data or using another installation can create a new identity. It is not a verified Blizzard account identifier.

Command examples: `/hg bounty shared receive on`, `/hg bounty shared publish on`, `/hg bounty shared threshold 1000`, `/hg bounty shared add 500 2h target`, `/hg bounty shared claim NAME`, `/hg bounty shared withdraw NAME`, `/hg bounty shared sync`. Substitute `/hgb` or `/hgn bounty` for `/hg bounty` in the standalone addons.

## Macros

**Macros** (`/hg macros`) holds ready-made PvP and PvE macros for your class and game, and a builder. Generated preset spell names resolve through spell IDs into your client's language when the spell is available; player-written scripts and imported bodies are kept as entered. The same window ships on its own as [Highground Macros](../../HighgroundMacros/README.md) (`/hgm`).

A macro puts one or more written game commands on an action-bar button. Saving one does not use the ability; you must drag its icon to an action bar and press it yourself.

- **Players (PvP)** is for fighting other players; **World (PvE)** is for quests, dungeons and raids. **Spell ranks** appears on clients with ranked spells.
- **For my class** starts with presets whose spells you know. **Utility** has macros for every class; **All classes** lets you browse others. Search filters the current tab and clears when you switch tabs.
- **My macros** contains your saved macros. **Create** opens the builder.

### Preset cards

1. Read the macro's one-line purpose and choose a target from the full-name menu (hover it to learn what the target means): for example **Focus**, **Mouseover**, **Target**, **Arena enemy 1** or a party member. All supported targets are in that menu. A focus is a second target: select a player and type `/focus` first.
2. Click **Use macro** to save it for this character. **Spell not learned** means a required spell is absent from your spellbook. Saving is disabled during combat.
3. Click **Open WoW macros**, then drag the saved icon onto an action bar. The message after saving repeats this next step.

A saved preset shows **Edit macro** and opens your existing text, preserving manual changes. **More** offers **Customize macro**, **Save for all characters** and, when a character copy exists, an explicit **Replace saved macro with this preset**. All-character copies use WoW's General macro storage; character copies use Character storage. Changing the target menu previews a version without rewriting a saved macro. Hover a card for its explanation. **Show macro code** at the bottom of the preset browser adds highlighted source to every preset card and remembers your choice. The preview follows the selected target; longer macros scroll inside the card. **Hide macro code** returns to the compact view. The preview shows the preset; **Edit macro** opens any saved manual changes.

### Create and edit

The setup and editor scroll independently. Inside the Highground window, the builder reclaims the duplicate title area to leave more room for your macro.

1. **Choose an action**: **Cast spell**, **Use item**, **Sequence** (`/castsequence`) or **Random** (`/castrandom`), then the spell, item or slot. Separate several spells with commas. In games with spell ranks, **Rank** pins a rank.
2. **Choose a target**: a menu names each target in full, including party members and the arena enemies available in your game. **Normal spell targeting** restores ordinary targeting. **A named group member** reveals a player-name field. The checkbox directly below the selected unit changes the recipient to that unit's target: **Use your focus's target** produces `@focustarget`, for example. It also works with mouseover, party and arena units, your current target, or a named group member; your own target stays selected. The recipient summary updates with the choice. Ground-at-cursor and self casts do not offer this option.
3. **Review & save**: edit or paste macro text directly, read **What this will do**, then choose **This character** (Character slots) or **All characters** (General slots) and **Save macro**. An existing name changes the action to **Update macro** and identifies the destination before saving. **Open WoW macros** stays at the bottom, so you can drag the saved macro to an action bar.

**Target conditions**, **Stance or form**, **Casting options**, **Alternative action**, **Use items first**, **Announce** and **Utility action** unfold when needed. Their headers count active options even while folded.

- **Target conditions**: living-enemy/ally checks and fallbacks. A fallback runs when the earlier macro conditions fail, including a missing recipient; range, line of sight and cooldowns do not trigger it. A self fallback is unavailable when the normal-target fallback already covers every remaining case.
- **Stance or form** (classes with stances or forms): in another stance, a press switches first (`[nostance:3] Berserker Stance`); press again to cast.
- **Casting options**: **Stop my current cast first**, **Stop casting after spell**, **Never cut my channel** (`/stopmacro [channeling]`), an explicit auto-attack choice, toggles, cancelling buffs and, for Hunters, Warlocks and Death Knights, a pet order: attack the spell's enemy or follow you. **Stop casting after spell** starts off and adds `/stopcasting` at the end for **Cast spell**. It attempts to cancel a normal cast after trying the spell; it cannot check Nightfall or guarantee instant-only casting. Normal casts may continue, and existing casts or channels can be interrupted. The editor warns about this ordering. Test while stationary with and without the proc before relying on it.
- **Alternative action**: one button, two jobs. When Shift, Ctrl or Alt is held, when right-clicked or out of combat, the macro casts the spell on yourself, on your focus or at your cursor, casts another spell at the same targets, or, in games with ranks, a lower rank (`[mod:shift] Healing Wave(Rank 3); Healing Wave`). A rank beside the same spell without one is not flagged.
- **Use items first**: both trinket slots, comma-separated names or slot numbers, and gear to equip first, such as a shield for Shield Bash (name a one-handed weapon before the shield).
- **Announce**: a party, raid, instance, say or yell line after the cast. `%t` names your target and `%f` your focus; the default names the spell's recipient when it can. It is sent with every press, even when the spell fails. During boss encounters only group chat works.
- Choosing a sequence reveals its reset settings.

Lines you type by hand survive recipe changes. **Manual edits** identifies text that differs from the recipe; the explanation always describes the text being saved, and warns that the form may not reflect those edits. An empty macro is a draft; errors appear once there is text to check. Validation and explanations have their own scroll area, and the save controls stay visible. Saving is disabled for errors or during combat, with a visible reason. Loading a different macro clears the old recipe and selects that macro's save destination.

### My macros

Your existing macros show their storage destination and flag problems as **Needs editing**. **Edit macro** opens the saved text in **Create**. **More** offers copying to the other destination and **Export macro**. **Export all** and **Import** appear on this tab. **Open WoW macros** opens Blizzard's window.

Imports open a per-macro review: existing names start **Skipped**; choose **Replace**, **Skip** or **Rename**, then **Import selected**. Names, bodies and available slots are checked before writing. **Undo changes** (`/hg macros history`) keeps the last 40 addon writes, including imports and rank updates. Undo restores the previous body or removes a macro just created by Highground. Restore the newest change first; later manual edits are protected. The history shows this character’s backups for the current faction and realm/ruleset, plus shared General macro backups. Character macros must be restored in their original context, outside combat.

### Spell ranks (WoW Forever)

Highground Macros has the same on Classic Era and The Burning Crusade. A spell name without a rank, like `/cast Frostbolt`, casts your highest rank. Every preset is written that way. Action buttons and macros that name a rank, like `Frostbolt(Rank 3)`, stay on it.

- **Highest spell rank** (Features, on by default): when you learn a new rank, action buttons on the rank it replaces move up to it. Buttons on lower ranks, kept on purpose to save mana, stay. **Also in macros** does the same for `Name(Rank N)` in your macros. Changes wait until you leave combat.
- **Spell ranks** (next to Players and World) lists a preset for every spell in your spellbook that has ranks. Each casts the spell by name, so it always uses your highest rank, also after you learn a new one. Heals and buffs go to the ally under your mouse, then your target, then you; other spells to your target, with focus and mouseover variants. `/hg macros presets` opens it.
- `/hg macros ranks` moves every button and macro on a lower rank up to your highest rank.
- Create has a **Rank** field: empty casts your highest rank; a number pins that rank. The checker warns when a macro pins a rank lower than one you know.

### Checks and autocomplete

Every macro is explained line by line in plain English. Problems are listed by line as you type, such as an unknown condition (`[@fokus]`), a missing `]`, more than 255 characters, a spell not in your spellbook, or a command your game lacks (`/ping` on Cataclysm). Macros with errors cannot be copied.

Autocomplete covers spells, items and slots, buffs to remove, commands, conditions and units, values such as `mod:shift`, raid markers (`/tm`), sequence resets (`reset=`) and `#showtooltip`. **Tab** accepts a suggestion; without an open list it moves between visible fields (Shift-Tab goes backwards). Completion appears beside the caret, above or below the current line, within the editor, so it does not cover the explanation. **Escape** dismisses completion first and leaves the editor on a second press. In the one-line fields, **Enter** also accepts and the arrow keys move through the list; in the macro box only **Tab** accepts, so **Enter** and the arrow keys keep their editing meaning.

## Teammate pressure shields (Cata)

Shield badges mark up to three teammates (`/hg pressure count N`, 1–3) with the most observed enemies targeting them. The number counts distinct enemies targeting that teammate, not damage. The shield turns from amber to red at three. Badges are private to you. Turn on friendly nameplates (usually Shift+V) to see them; your own badge sits left of the player frame when your nameplate is hidden.
