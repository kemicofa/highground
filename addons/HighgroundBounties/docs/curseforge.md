# Highground Bounties — Personal and Guild Bounties

Highground Bounties lets you put a bounty on any player and never miss them again. The moment a bounty target shows up, you hear it, see it and know where they are. Bring your guild in and the whole guild hunts together. It runs on Retail, WoW Forever and Cataclysm Classic.

## Features

### 💰 Bounty alerts

- Put a bounty on anyone, with or without gold: `/hgb add 500 target`.
- When the target shows up as your target, your mouseover or on a nameplate, you get a **sound** and a **center-screen alert**, anywhere: open world, battlegrounds and arenas.
- A **gold coin** sits beside their nameplate with the reward under it, and their tooltip names the bounty.
- Optional: tell your party or raid when you spot one.

### 📜 The bounty board

- Every bounty as a wanted card: reward, status, who placed it, and where and when it was last seen.
- Tabs for **ALL**, **MINE**, **GUILD** and **PENDING**, a search box and the guild purse.
- Add a bounty straight from your target. Open the board with `/hgb`, a key binding or a player's right-click menu (Retail and WoW Forever).

### 🛡️ Guild bounties

- Submit a bounty to your guild. An officer approves it, then every member gets it.
- Open-world sightings are shared with the guild, and on Cata your killing blow on a target tells the guild.
- Works together with guild members who use **Highground**.
- Highground Bounties keeps the list; players pay the gold themselves.

### 👀 Nearby enemies

- An open-world list of hostile players you have seen, most recent first, with level, guild and zone.
- Left-click targets, right-click focuses. Bounty targets get a gold frame.
- **Stealth alerts (new):** a chat line when a nearby enemy uses Stealth, Prowl, Vanish, Invisibility, Shadowmeld or Camouflage, and when it fades.
- **Enemy history (new):** every enemy you meet is remembered, with kills both ways. Someone you met before gets a chat line and `met 3x  K1 D2` on their row. `/hgb nearby forget` wipes it.

### 🎨 Themes

- **Highground** (dark navy and teal), **Ember** (charcoal, crimson and amber) or **Parchment** (light paper with the classic WoW font): `/hgb theme NAME`.

## Getting started

- Type `/hgb` to open the bounty board, `/hgb config` for settings and `/hgb help` for every command.
- Already using **Highground**? It has bounties built in (`/hg bounty`). Install Highground Bounties on its own if you only want the bounties.
