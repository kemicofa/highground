Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: the text-free cinematic background layer for a very slim Highground World of Warcraft PvP Twitch stream overlay.
Generate a wide 1920 x 640 image, 3:1 landscape. It will be cropped to ONLY the central horizontal strip from y=176 to y=464, so all useful lighting must be in that shallow middle band. Composition: left 18 percent of the strip has a soft brilliant cobalt blue halo and a few tiny warm gold sparks behind a separate 3D gold crest that will be composited later; center and right 80 percent are extremely dark midnight navy / obsidian forged steel with a subtle brushed texture and scarcely visible engraved topographic contour lines. A warm glancing gold light runs very subtly along the lower edge of the shallow center strip. Far right has a faint warm amber reflection. The backdrop must feel expensive, polished, dimensional, magical and cinematic, inspired by the navy/gold fantasy battlefield palette. Broad quiet negative space across the middle and right for highly readable marketing copy, with uniform dark values. Avoid loud details. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no characters, no crest, no symbols, no buttons, no UI, no frame, no border, no circle outline, no visible sun, no checkerboard, no mockup. Opaque background. This is a finished production bitmap texture, not a concept sheet.
