Highground battleground alert sounds

alert-capture.ogg (a quick two-tone siren), alert-objective.ogg (a falling
horn call), alert-resurrection.ogg (a rising bell arpeggio) and
alert-sap.ogg (two muted plucks and a tick) are original synthesized cues.
They contain no game audio or third-party samples and are covered by this
repository's MIT license.

Regenerate with tools/sounds/alerts_source.py (Python 3, numpy and ffmpeg).
